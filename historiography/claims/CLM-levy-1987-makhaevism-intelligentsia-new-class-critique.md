## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
波兰裔俄国异端理论家扬·瓦茨拉夫·马哈伊斯基（Jan Wacław Machajski, 1866–1926）早于吉拉斯（Djilas）与古尔德纳（Gouldner）数十年，基于俄国社会民主工党的经验提出了关于脑力劳动者“新阶级”（New Class）最严密的政治经济学批判：他指出白领与专业知识分子的高薪本质上是剥削体力工人劳动所积累的“知识资本”（intellectual capital）的回报，并直接将马克思主义定性为新兴脑力劳动者阶级利用工人运动夺权、在国有化或扩张的国家机器中建立“专家与官职持有者”特权统治的阶级意识形态；这一将城市无产阶级视为炸毁旧政权的“人肉炸药”（human dynamite）并在1917年后借内战工人凋零建立党国知识分子机器（party intelligentsia apparat）的机制，构成了后来俄国围绕“工人监督”与“工人反对派”抗争的深层结构根源。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **“知识资本”（Intellectual Capital）的剥削本质**：David Beetham（第4章）与 Carl Levy（第1章导论）指出，马哈伊斯基从经济基础上剖析了脑力劳动与体力劳动的分工，认为知识分子在大学中获得的文凭与专业知识是由体力工人在工厂中的血汗劳动供养的；知识分子向资本家或国家出卖文凭所获取的级差高薪（differential salary），实质上是“知识资本”对体力工人的剥削回报（阿尔文·古尔德纳后来将其同化为现代人力资本与文化资本理论）。
2. **揭露马克思主义作为“新知识分子阶级”的意识形态**：不同于自居“真正马克思主义者”的西欧工团主义者，马哈伊斯基将马克思主义本身谴责为新知识分子阶级的宗教与意识形态。他论证说，“社会主义科学必然性”教义使体力工人陷入被动等待，而俄国社会民主党人将社会主义革命推迟到遥远未来、坚称“资产阶级革命必须先行”，实则是站在资产阶级宪政主义一边，企图借劳工运动巩固脑力劳动者在国家机器中的统治地位。
3. **集产主义（Collectivism）与国家主义（Statism）的剩余产品占有机制**：Stephen Yeo（第7章）指出，巴枯宁关于马克思主义掌权将建立“国家工程师组成的特权科学—政治阶级”的论点，在马哈伊斯基著作中获得了最严密的表述；在废除生产资料私有制的“集产主义”与“国家主义”体制下，受教育阶层依然保留了对“专门知识/技能”（know-how / expertise）的个人垄断所有权，以及党国“官职持有者”（office holders）的集中化行政权，从而通过不等额工资、特权与津贴占有社会剩余产品。
4. **1917年后俄国“东方新阶级”的生成与史学辨析**：Carl Levy（第8章结论）总结“东方新阶级”理论指出，俄国知识分子将年轻的城市工人阶级当作炸毁沙皇亚细亚国家大厦的“人肉炸药”，并在1917年后随着革命工人在内战与饥荒中的大量凋零，建立起自我永续的党内知识分子政治机器（party intelligentsia apparat）。同时，Levy 援引 Sirianni（1982）与 S. A. Smith（1983）关于十月革命后工厂委员会与“工人监督”（workers' control）的实证研究，指出必须区分马哈伊斯基/Szelényi 式的“知识分子技术专家新阶级”与吉拉斯（Djilas）笔下1930年代由工农出身年轻斯大林主义者构成的“白领党务官僚（apparatchiks）新阶级”。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
> "Critics of socialism as a vehicle for this ‘New Class’ included Bakunin, who apparently coined the phrase, and J. W. Machajski (1866-1926), an eccentric Russo-Polish Marxist theorist whose concept of intellectual capital Alvin Gouldner assimilated to recent theories of human, cultural and symbolic capitals." `[PDF p. 32 / p. 17]`
> （出处：`sources/monographs/levy_1987_socialism_intelligentsia/ch04_introduction_historical_and_theoretical.md#L712-L717`）

> "Another who did the same was the Pole Jan Machajski, who reached broadly similar conclusions from his experience of Russian Social Democracy. He also developed a number of distinctive emphases of his own. One concerned the economic basis of the division between intellectual and manual labour. He argued that the differential salary commanded by the white-collared and professional strata constituted the return to a specific form of capital, intellectual capital, which depended upon the manual worker for its creation:
> ‘The intellectual brings to market his knowledge which has been acquired thanks to the labour of the workers, just like the capitalist factory; while he studies at university . . . the workers sweat it out in the factory, producing the means for his education. . . . The diploma which he sells to the capitalist has been acquired from their exploitation.’15
> Like the syndicalists, Machajski saw these intellectual strata using the labour movement to consolidate a position for themselves within an expanded bourgeois state. But where the syndicalists claimed to be the true defenders of the Marxist tradition against the ‘statist’ distortions of Social Democracy, Machajski denounced Marxism as itself the ideology of the new intellectual class. Its doctrine of the scientific inevitability of socialism, he argued, lulled the workers into passivity, and enabled their leaders to preach revolution while practising reform and supporting measures of state expansion. Even the Social Democratic revolutionaries in Russia, by postponing the socialist revolution to the distant future, and arguing that the bourgeois revolution must come first, had put themselves on the side of bourgeois constitutionalism.16" `[PDF pp. 128-129 / pp. 113-114]`
> （出处：`sources/monographs/levy_1987_socialism_intelligentsia/ch09_reformism_the_bourgeoisification_of_the.md#L328-L373`）

> "For Bakunin, Marxism in power would organise society ‘under the direct command of state engineers who will constitute a new privileged scientific-political class’. This thesis had ‘its most consistent and rigorous formulation’ in the work of J. W. Machajski (1866-1926).1" `[PDF p. 235 / p. 220]`
> （出处：`sources/monographs/levy_1987_socialism_intelligentsia/ch15_notes_on_three_socialisms_collectivism_s.md#L33-L39`）

> "The young working class became a form of human dynamite, a tool capable of demolishing the edifice of the Asiatic state. After 1917 the politically successful elements of the intelligentsia laid the foundations for a ‘New Class’. The decimation of revolutionary workers during civil war and famine, and the Stalinist purges of errant colleagues and rebellious peasants, allowed this party intelligentsia to create its own self-perpetuating political apparat which guaranteed its existence as a distinct class in Soviet society." `[PDF p. 288 / p. 273]`
> （出处：`sources/monographs/levy_1987_socialism_intelligentsia/ch18_conclusion_historiography_and_the_new_cl.md#L103-L116`）

## 语境说明与局限提示
上述引文分别来自 Carl Levy 主编《Socialism and the Intelligentsia 1880–1914》的第1章（Levy 导论）、第4章（David Beetham 论改良主义与考茨基/列宁灌输论的对立面）、第7章（Stephen Yeo 论集产主义、国家主义与联合主义）及第8章（Levy 结论）。需注意：Levy 本人在第8章结论中对目的论式的“东方新阶级”宿命论持审慎的修正态度，强调1917年前布尔什维克、孟什维克与社会革命党知识分子之间存在真实的战略分裂，且1930年代斯大林体制下的党务官僚（Djilas 所说的出身工农的 apparatchiks）实际上清洗并取代了1917年革命时期的老布尔什维克知识分子与旧技术专家。此外，该书重在1880–1914年思想谱系与1917年后宏观社会史的勾连，对1920–1921年俄共（布）党内“工人反对派”具体派别运作的微观档案并未直接展开，而是通过引证 Marshall Shatz（1968）关于马哈伊斯基主义的博士论文及 Sirianni（1982）、S. A. Smith（1983）关于十月革命后工人监督的研究来建立理论关联。
