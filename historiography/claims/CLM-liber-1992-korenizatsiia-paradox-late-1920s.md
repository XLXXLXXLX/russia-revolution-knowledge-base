## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
利伯指出，1920 年代中后期 **本土化（korenizatsiia）/乌克兰化** 在弥合俄罗斯化城市与非俄罗斯农村裂痕、塑造苏维埃乌克兰工人—知识精英的同时，制造了 **制度化语言差异与“难以想象共同体”式读者公共圈**；该进程在削弱俄罗斯文化霸权的同时，也 **正当化并延续语言冲突**，使民族维权意识在中央可控范围外自发滋长——至 1928 年前后成为赫维廖维、沃洛布耶夫等“民族共产主义”挑战的社会—意识形态土壤。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **政策初衷与机制**：作为统一语言—文化—人事政策，本土化在工业化混乱期提升非俄罗斯语言声望，确立多种官方语言的社会基础，从而“制度化与合法化了语言冲突，并维持、延续了它” `[pp. 155–156]`。
2. **城市身份震荡**：乌克兰农民大规模入城瓦解旧有身份纽带，催生矛盾的城市“心理国度”；乌克兰化（1925 年卡冈诺维奇加强）通过母语教育、扫盲与出版革命扩大乌克兰语读者群体，将城乡乌克兰人整合进想象的民族共同体 `[pp. 156–161]`。
3. **1928 年张力外溢**：赫维廖维警告 NEP 社会进程将导致俄乌“两种文化”冲突，要求党站在“年轻乌克兰社会”一边；沃洛布耶夫论证乌克兰仍遭联盟财政—计划“汲取”，被斥为鼓吹“两种经济斗争”。斯大林 1926 年已指责乌克兰化可能脱离“全苏联文化”而反莫斯科 `[pp. 179–189; 192–194]`。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
> "As a unified policy on language, culture and personnel, korenizatsiia sought to legitimise multiculturalism within the Soviet Union while avoiding the creation of multiple centres of power. During the upheavals provoked by industrialisation, the implementation of this policy raised the prestige of non-Russian languages and cultures and created the necessary social base for multiculturalism. The establishment of several official languages and the social constituencies to support them assured 'a long-term, if not permanent, linguistic split'. In effect, korenizatsiia 'institutionalised and legitimised linguistic conflict, thereby maintaining and perpetuating it'." `[pp. 155–156]`

> "The social upheavals caused by Ukrainisation and industrialisation strengthened non-Russian national consciousness and allowed it to develop spontaneously outside the control of the central authorities. The problem was not so much the rise of national consciousness as the emergence of an increasingly spontaneous and difficult-to-control national assertiveness." `[p. 157]`

> "The social processes set in train by NEP logically lead to a conflict between two cultures. Ukrainian society, once it has been strengthened, will never consent to remaining, in law as in fact, the subordinate party—the Russian competitor. We cannot go too far with empty phrases. Our task is to stop this conflict. In other words: we must immediately take the side of the active young Ukrainian society, which represents not only the peasantry but also the workers." `[p. 179]` *(Khvyliovyi, 1926; English rendering in Liber 1992 / Chinese edition `ch11_part_2`.)*

## 语境说明与局限提示
“悖论”为札记层概念概括，利伯正文以经验叙述呈现 **政策目标（整合）** 与 **非意图后果（民族公共领域扩大）** 的张力。1923 年“民族契约”与十二大本土化决议（`ch04`，`pp. 55–60`）提供制度起点；本卡聚焦 **迟 1920 年代城市化加速后** 的矛盾激化。勿将赫维廖维—沃洛布耶夫批评等同于全盘否定 1923–1927 乌克兰化成就；1933 年后政策急转及大饥荒维度须另卡处理，且超出本精读下限（1929 年底）。
