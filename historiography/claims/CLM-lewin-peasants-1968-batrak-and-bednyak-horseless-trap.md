## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
莫舍·勒温指出，1917–1920 年土地均分并未伴随耕畜与农具的均等分配，导致新经济政策（NEP）时期约占全苏农户 30% 的无马贫农（`bednyaki`）与约 300 万雇农（`batraki`）陷入结构性依附陷阱：缺乏役畜与农具的贫农不得不向较殷实的邻户高价租借耕畜，或被迫将自己分得的份地出租给装备齐全的当家农户（`khozyain`）并反过来受雇于人；与此同时，频繁的家庭析产（`razdel`）不断将原本勉强自给的中农拆分推入贫农行列，彻底证伪了苏联官方所谓新经济政策下贫农持续上升为中农的“中农化（`oserednyachivanie`）”宣传神话。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **无马缺具引发的反向土地流转与隐蔽依附**：据中央统计局（TsSU）1926 年前后的估算，俄罗斯联邦 28.2%、乌克兰 37.5%、白俄罗斯 11.4% 的农户须靠租借役畜与农具才能耕种份地（`p. 44`）。无马户（`bezloshadnye`，占全苏农户约 30%，约 600–700 万户，`p. 53`）往往以收成分成偿付耕马租金，或将份地私下转租给富裕农户再去打短工。
2. **雇农沦为“十月革命后社会的贱民”**：全苏雇农人数由 1927 年的 276 万增至 1929 年的 323.1 万，其中缺乏法律保障的日工占比由 20% 激增至 39%，且 1926 年仍有 63% 的雇农拥有微薄份地（`pp. 49–50`）。农林工人工会（`Vserabotzemles`）在 300 万雇农中仅组织起 424,621 人，1927 年底雇农党员仅 1.4 万人；政权既未邀请雇农加入集体农庄，也无力为其配备农具，使其在长达 12–13 小时的盘剥劳动中沦为被遗忘的底层（`pp. 51–52`）。
3. **家庭析产（`razdel`）与“中农化”神话的破产**：莫洛托夫在十五大不得不承认农业税免税贫农比例已由 27% 扩大至 35%，且农村贫农工作遭到“丑恶的忽视”（`pp. 55–56`）。勒温证明，中农总数之所以表面未减，仅是因为农户频繁分家析产使大批中农家庭在保留形式分类的同时实际滑向贫困，信贷合作社资金亦主要流向富裕农户（获贷率 36.1%，贫农仅 5.8%–18.7%，`pp. 56–59`）。

## 原文核验引文（Verbatim Quotes）
> "This situation created a veritable tangle of contradictions, which caused the re-emergence of the differences which had so recently been evened out. A peasant who had no draught animals, or lacked sufficient agricultural implements to cultivate his land, had to hire them from a more fortunate neighbour, or else was forced to let part of his land for rent to a better-equipped khozyain, and then seek employment as a labourer under the same neighbour, or elsewhere." `[p. 44 | ch04_2_the_problem_of_class_stratification_wi.md#L157-L164]`

> "We know that the batrak aspired, like every other peasant, to become an independent farmer, to have more land, and more implements with which to cultivate it. He would perhaps have been prepared to join an agricultural collective, but no one invited him to join such a collective, the regime at that time not being in the least interested in collectivization. Nor did anyone think of providing the batraks with more land and implements. The NEP economy needed the batraks, but the Soviet regime could not find the way to deal with their predicament. And so, for the moment, the batrak continued to figure as the pariah of the society which had emerged from the October Revolution, and to wait for his share in the fruits of that revolution." `[p. 52 | ch04_2_the_problem_of_class_stratification_wi.md#L564-L575]`

> "If the numbers of serednyaks were not decreasing, it is because they were being reinforced by frequent divisions of households whose members, while formally continuing to be classified as serednyaks, were in fact becoming poorer as a result of these divisions. Many bednyak farms were disappearing, but others came to replace them from the ranks of the serednyaks. The general impression is rather one of an increase in the category of bednyaks, and a slight decrease in that of the serednyaks." `[p. 57 | ch04_2_the_problem_of_class_stratification_wi.md#L785-L793]`

## 语境说明与局限提示（Context & Caveats）
本论断出自第一编第 1–2 章对新经济政策末期农村社会分层的解剖。勒温同时强调，尽管农村存在上述因农具缺口引发的租借与雇工依附，但当时苏联农民总体仍高度均质，全苏雇佣 1 名以上雇农的农户仅占 1%，且在国家粮价与工业品价格问题上，贫农作为小粮食生产者往往表现出与中农完全一致的市场反应（`pp. 50, 54`）。
