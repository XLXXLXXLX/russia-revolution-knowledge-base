## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
> **作者归属特别辨正**：在本论文集（*Making Workers Soviet*, Cornell UP, 1994）中，标题为 *“From Working Class to Urban Laboring Mass: On Politics and Social Categories in the Formative Years of the Soviet System”* 的第 9 章（`orig. pp. 253–273` / `PDF pp. 270–290`）实际作者为**加博尔·T·里特斯波恩（Gábor T. Rittersporn）**；而**摩西·莱文（Moshe Lewin）**撰写的实为全书结语第 13 章 *“Concluding Remarks”*（`orig. pp. 376–389` / `PDF pp. 393–406`）。莱文在 `p. 380` 专门点名评议里特斯波恩“工人阶级沦为城市劳动大众”的假说，二者构成了既相互呼应又存在理论张力的二重论断。

**摩西·莱文（Ch. 13）的核心命题**：莱文批评部分社会史/解构派学者过度消解“阶级”概念导致工人在研究视野中“消失”，主张必须在“流动性（fluidity）”中重申**“自在阶级（*an sich*）”与支配关系（domination）的客观实在性**；他指出苏维埃国家一面用将职员纳入“无产阶级”或“两个友好阶级加一个知识分子阶层（*prosloika*）”的**“神话社会学（mythological sociology）”**掩盖由脱胎于农民父权底座与重工业专制结合而成的**“行政阶级（administrative class）”**统治（`pp. 378–383`），一面在大工业化涌入海量低教育程度非熟练苦力（*chernorabochie*）后制造出受配给、住房与“你（*Ty*）/您（*Vy*）”等级礼仪规训的**粗放劳动力群体 *rabsila*（“a crude labor force, rather than a working class”）**（`pp. 384–389`）。而在同卷第 9 章中，**里特斯波恩**从政治决策垄断权视角切入，指出由于生产关系无法决定社会地位、且工人与农民同样被剥夺独立代表机构，大转变后苏联工人至多只是一群在“城市劳作大众（urban toiling mass）”层面上适应制度约束的群体，而非政治意义上的“劳动阶级”（`pp. 257, 273`）。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **对“阶级消失论”的矫正与“支配关系”重构（Lewin, `Ch. 13`, `orig. pp. 377–381`）**：
   - 驳斥将历史还原为纯粹“方法论个人主义”或因文化/道德叙事丰富而放逐“阶级”的做法；援引 S. A. Smith，认为“支配（domination）”比狭义剩余价值“剥削”更能涵盖“老板/官员（*chinovniki*）vs. 我们（*nash brat*）”的真实压迫；工人喊出 *“nas za liudei ne schitaiut”*（“他们不把我们当人看”）正是阶级现实的表达，即便尚未用正统马克思主义词汇或政党纲领（*für sich*）表述（`pp. 377–380`）。
   - 在 `p. 380` 逐章点评全书时，莱文将里特斯波恩“工人阶级变成‘城市劳动大众（an urban laboring mass）’”的推论归入“值得商榷、但属同一问题域（disputable, no doubt, but belonging to the same register）”的流动性观察：社会实体确实会在激进工业化与乡村移民浪潮中分解与重组，但“流动性（‘bubbly’ qualities）”并不取消阶级作为现实过程的作用（`pp. 380–381`）。
2. **从“隐蔽的阶级”到“神话社会学”与行政统治阶级（Lewin, `Ch. 13`, `orig. pp. 381–383`）**：
   - 对话奥洛夫斯基（Ch. 8）：指出将 *sluzhashchie* 塞进“无产阶级”统计口径起初出于“无产阶级国家所雇皆无产者”的意识形态推论（以显得人数壮大），此后便恶性膨胀为彻底脱离现实、扼杀一切诚实社会学研究的**斯大林主义“神话社会学”**（如“两个非对抗阶级加一个 *prosloika*”公式）。这一话语魔术真正要遮掩的，是脱离并凌驾于工农之上的“行政阶级（*vysshii komandnyi sostav*, *otvet-rabotniki*）”掌权事实（`pp. 381–383`）。
3. **没有资本家的无产阶级：*Chernorabochie*、*Rabsila* 与“长官同志（*Comrade Nachal'nik*）”等级制（Lewin, `Ch. 13`, `orig. pp. 383–389`）**：
   - 解释“有类资本主义工业无产阶级却无古典资产阶级”的谜题：斯大林体制实为“俄国农村深厚父权社会现实与当时烟囱工业专制特征的‘联姻’”（`p. 384`）。1940 年官方统计工业基本岗位中 *chernorabochie*（无技术苦力）仍高达 `65%`（辅助岗位达 `95%`），劳动者普遍仅受三至四年小学教育（1926 年普查仅问识字率 *gramotnost'* 而不问学历 *obrazovanie*）（`pp. 384–385`）。
   - 配给制（1928–1929 起）、窝棚/住房分配与低廉劳动力过剩共同造就了莱文所称的 ***rabsila*（粗放劳动力，而非真正的工人阶级）**；企业以公司城镇（company town）式福利维系人身依附，而上级对工人动辄以粗鄙单数“你（*Ty*）”相呼（*tykat'*）、工人却须对“长官同志”敬称“您（*Vy*）”，暴露了超越具体生产关系的深层专制支配结构（`pp. 385–389`）。
4. **对照：里特斯波恩（`Ch. 9`, `orig. pp. 253–273`）的“政治决定论式社会分层”**：
   - 里特斯波恩以 1928–1932 年新工人/职员中 `68%` 来自农村（1933–1937 年 `54%`）、城市流入 `7,700 万`/流出 `6,000 万`人次的剧烈流徙（`pp. 256, 267 n.57`）以及“计划中的计划消失”为背景，论证在苏联**政治决策垄断权而非生产关系**才构成唯一“政治阶级（统治精英）”与无组织群众的分界线；即便存在特定车间文化或对立情绪，缺乏独立利益代表机制与反精英政治认知，使战前工人总体上仍只是“城市劳作大众（urban toiling mass）”而非政治意义上的“劳动阶级”（`pp. 257, 272–273`）。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
### Ⅰ. 摩西·莱文（Moshe Lewin, Ch. 13 "Concluding Remarks"）
> "So, if we drop any explicit or implicit claims that classes and 'structures' are immutable, if we accept that social realities can be, and often are, very fluid, then the concept of class becomes real and useful, but our work becomes more intricate because we have ceased to take class and class consciousness for granted." `[orig. p. 378 | PDF p. 395 | ch18_13_concluding_remarks.md#L123-L128]`

> "Thus, from chapter to chapter, the squads of workers described change their composition, attitudes, politics, and ideology: Zelnik's 1870s versus the 1890s; Hogan's metallisty before and during the revolution; Kuromiya's miners, a non-classy thing par excellence, and yet a political power keg ready to fight like no other more 'classy' layers did; Koenker's printers, highly articulate and ready for socialism, entering into a battle for sheer survival against the Communists as they probably would against anyone else in certain conditions, and accepting the same Communists in other circumstances. Let us add Gabor Rittersporn's supposition about his working class becoming 'an urban laboring mass'—disputable, no doubt, but belonging to the same register." `[orig. p. 380 | PDF p. 397 | ch18_13_concluding_remarks.md#L202-L212]`

> "Here already is an instance of the tendency to use class terminology to mystify, which led at a later stage to a full-blown 'mythological sociology,' a key trait of the Stalinist period, a practice that made social theory entirely devoid of any relation to life and destroyed the social sciences altogether. Stalin's 'two friendly classes and one layer' (prosloika), or the quite unsuccessful effort to drape the poorly schooled mass of Soviet employees of the 1930s in the toga of the supposedly massive 'intelligentsia,' instead of their previous inclusion in the proletariat, are examples of such a mythical sociology." `[orig. pp. 381–382 | PDF pp. 398–399 | ch18_13_concluding_remarks.md#L262-L275]`

> "The low cost of labor favored keeping labor surpluses, the surpluses hindered raising professional standards, and low standards meant low labor productivity. All these factors together contributed to the appearance of the phenomenon I call rabsila—a crude labor force, rather than a working class, that the managers treated as such unless labor shortages forced them to act differently." `[orig. p. 385 | PDF p. 402 | ch18_13_concluding_remarks.md#L441-L446]`

> "But 'Vy' reappeared in official circles and, at the same time, 'Ty' became the way a boss talked to a worker or other lower ranks, whereas the boss was addressed respectfully by them as 'Vy.' These terms are no trifle. They are not just expressions of relative status and power, but also of an authoritarianism in a civilization that did not depart enough from the culture of crude domination, wielded directly and potently so that there were no misunderstandings." `[orig. pp. 387–388 | PDF pp. 404–405 | ch18_13_concluding_remarks.md#L544-L559]`

### Ⅱ. 对照直引：加博尔·T·里特斯波恩（Gábor T. Rittersporn, Ch. 9 "From Working Class to Urban Laboring Mass"）
> "Moreover, every relation it maintained with society at large was a political relationship, which seems to explain why the peacefulness of these relations or the economic soundness of these actions were secondary to the defense of the decision-making monopoly. ... Seen from this angle, workers comprised part of a category situated at the antipode of the elite of decision makers, the only political class of the system. Despite the notable differences between the occupational status and self-image of industrial labor and that of the farm population, this category included the peasantry that was also excluded from direct access to the sphere of action of the administrative hierarchy." `[orig. pp. 257–258 | PDF pp. 274–275 | ch14_9_from_working_class_to_urban_laboring_m.md#L206-L249]`

> "If there are some indications that despite powerful countervailing influences, Soviet workers were by no means unaware of the advantages of independent interest representation, their ways of acting out their conflict with the regime are rather far from bespeaking an understanding of the nature and consequences of system-inherent constraints. Their adaptation to such constraints, when all is said and done, was characteristic of an urban toiling mass rather than a political class of labor." `[orig. p. 273 | PDF p. 290 | ch14_9_from_working_class_to_urban_laboring_m.md#L1003-L1010]`
