## 核心论断命题（Thesis）

乔治·亚尼认为，斯托雷平土地改革既非大臣们在 1905–1906 年按预设蓝图严密推进的产物，也非惊慌失措的临时拼凑，而是沙俄行政机关在 1905–1917 年间通过“干中学”演化出的制度实验。1905–1906 年维特、克里沃舍因与古尔科等人原拟以优惠售地及个体纸面确权逐步拆解村社，但在 1907–1908 年农民大规模集体请愿整村合并后，缺乏法定强制权的基层土地整理委员会与测量员依托实绩奖金机制在与农民的谈判中主动调适：至 1909–1910 年，改革重心已转向推进整村合并、团体土地整理、部分合并与保留中心聚落的 *otrub* 农场。亚尼据此否定“全面非法暴力强制论”，指出 1917 年倒退主要限于伏尔加河下游土地银行购地及零星单干户，村社整村整理实为官民行政互动的务实成果。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）

1. **非个人蓝图亦非临时拼凑（`pp. 275–279 [PDF pp. 2–6]`）**：主要法令由维特、克里沃舍因与古尔科在斯托雷平入阁前（1902–1904 年维特农业特别会议 58 卷证词、1903 年土地司草案基础上）拟定，`1905-11-03 [O.S.] / 1905-11-16 [N.S.]`（`1907-01-01 [O.S.] / 01-14 [N.S.]` 取消赎金）、`1906-03-04 [O.S.] / 03-17 [N.S.]` 与 `1906-11-09 [O.S.] / 11-22 [N.S.]` 三敕令均为待检验假说；克里沃舍因 1906 年 2 月呈沙皇备忘录坦言“必须先行动，行动本身会指明最佳路径”。
2. **从“拆散村社”向“整村现代化”的实践逆转（`pp. 282–286 [PDF pp. 9–13]`）**：农村习惯法悬置、中央对基层控制乏力（每名地方长官辖约 4 万农民）及单户碎裂至 40–50 块条田的复杂格局使初期官员不敢预期剧变。然而至 `1908-01-01 [O.S.] / 01-14 [N.S.]` 前即有约 76,000 户申请合村，次年再增 205,000 户（`p. 285` fn. 43），倒逼政府于 1909 年紧急扩训测量员。同时地方实践发现个体确权与单干反而阻挠整村合并（引 `1907-03-25 [O.S.] / 04-07 [N.S.]`《俄罗斯报》及 `1911-05-29 [O.S.] / 06-11 [N.S.]` 土地整理法），政府遂转向鼓励集体合作整理。
3. **基层激励机制与偏离中央预期的三种变通形式（`pp. 287–290 [PDF pp. 14–17]`）**：整村合并需耗时两个夏季且竣工前村民随时有权否决，而测量员奖金（1909 年 4 月规定）与地方长官晋升津贴均与“完成且耐用”挂钩。在此互动下，尽管圣彼得堡不断下令劝阻，地方仍普遍涌现：(a) 至 1914 年完成数反超整村合并的**团体土地整理**（*gruppovnoe zemleustroistvo*）；(b) 莫斯科周边盛行的**部分合并**；(c) 占据绝对主流的 **otrub** 农场（保留中心居住区），而政府理想中的独立农庄 **khutor** 始终不足全部合并案例的三分之一（引 Dubrowski 1929）。
4. **反驳“强制论”与 1917 年复归的地域实证（`pp. 291–293 [PDF pp. 18–20]`）**：承认 1905–1913 年骚乱与戒严背景下偶有暴力摩擦，但指出政府事先根本未预期整村合并，无从强迫；佩尔申（1922）与切尔内舍夫（1926）证实 1917 年强行回归三田制高度集中于下伏尔加各省（萨马拉、萨拉托夫、辛比尔斯克）及莫斯科以南农民银行向外村买主售地及零星单飞户，绝大多数整村合并项目安然无恙，团体整理更无遭破坏记录，且 1920 年代初即便在苏维埃默示反对下仍自发复兴。

## 原文核验引文（Verbatim Quotes）

> "The enabling decrees, chiefly those of November 3, 1905, March 4, 1906, and November 9, 1906, were hypotheses to be tested rather than the sole basis for the Reform's development, and the carrying out of the Reform was more a process of trial and error than the execution of a preconceived plan. In 1905-6, the tsar's ministers did not know what programs would be suitable to their ultimate purpose, and they had no reliable way of discovering such programs save by observing the results of what their executive agencies did." `[pp. 275–276 / PDF pp. 2–3; ch01_p_275.md#L35-L36, ch02_p_276.md#L9-L19]`

> "Accordingly, Krivoshein set up the Reform organization to operate as a service agency without statutory power. The local commissions could carry out only those programs which they could persuade the peasants to accept. The commissions' actual accomplishments, then, developed out of their interaction and negotiation with the peasants and did not simply emerge out of instructions from St. Petersburg. It was the experience of this interaction, more than any other factor, which produced the changes in the government's programs that occurred after 1906. What is now remembered as the Stolypin Land Reform emerged not from legislative enactments or high level policy decisions but rather from the executive operation of the government administration as a whole." `[p. 277 / PDF p. 4; ch03_p_277.md#L10-L27]`

> "By 1909, then, the government was encouraging the peasants to achieve consolidation in groups-cooperating among themselves rather than turning against one another. The Reform had begun as an attempt to hasten what was assumed to be the inevitable dissolution of the peasant communities but had evolved instead into a means for preserving them by modernizing their economic organization. This does not imply that government officials actually adopted a new political and social philosophy after 1906. There is no evidence that they consciously abandoned one set of convictions and picked up another. What changed was their action, not necessarily their thought, and thus the change must be observed first of all in what the government was doing." `[p. 286 / PDF p. 13; ch12_p_286.md#L40-L59]`

> "The point here is that the Reform did not originate in any place at any one time with any one man or group of men, nor did it arise full-blown from any conscious legislative process or from the class interests and intrigues that may have been behind any conscious legislative process. It emerged from the actual operation of an executive administration, an operation involving interaction between peasants and local officials on the one hand and between local officials and high-level statesmen on the other." `[p. 293 / PDF p. 20; ch19_p_293.md#L32-L41]`

## 语境说明与局限提示

亚尼此文是后来其专著 *The Urge to Mobilize* (1982) 的核心前驱，直接对抗冷战期欧美（Robinson, Owen）及苏联史学（Dubrovskii, Antonova）强调“沙俄暴力压迫与土改彻底失败”的共识。但该文刻意抽象掉高层宪政与宫廷贵族政治斗争——例如 1908–1909 年废除地方长官案及 1911 年西部地方自治局危机中联合贵族会与尼古拉二世对斯托雷平的掣肘（见 `CLM-ascher-2001-stolypin-agrarian-reform-political-impasse`），亦未充分考察非黑土区护照—财政压榨记忆（`CLM-burds-1998-passport-regime-and-commune-fiscal-coercion`）及村内多数决剥夺少数农户牧场与公地的微观社会摩擦。
