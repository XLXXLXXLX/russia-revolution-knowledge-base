## 核心论断命题（Thesis）

西格尔鲍姆与苏尼在导论中主张，研究苏联工人阶级不能将“阶级意识”与客观经济地位简单对应；阶级是**政治—文化假设**，其历史 inseparable from 关于阶级的论述性宣称。阶级身份在语言、制度（工会、户口式分类、宣传艺术）与车间实践中被不断固定、排斥与替换——因此“俄国工人”既是生产关系中的位置，更是被党国与他者争夺的**想象性共同体**。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）

1. **超越自在/自为二分**：援引 Joan Scott 与 Gareth Stedman Jones，指出利益与认同均在政治语言内部生成；历史任务应是描绘（例如熟练男工）身份如何被特权化并排斥替代方案（`pp. 337–343`）。
2. **帝国史叙事批判**： Sewell 呼吁将俄工人史从“发现阶级使命”的 teleology 改写为文化建构过程；1905 后社会民主党阶级话语对金属工经验尤具说服力（`pp. 9–11`）。
3. **苏联 MULTIVALENT 阶级话语**：1920 年代阶级语言用于区分苏维埃/资本主义国家、Proletkult/RAPP 文化战线、排斥 NEP 商贩与旧专家，并渗透至入学、入党与人口统计（`pp. 17–18`）。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）

> "Class instead becomes a political and cultural postulate, the assertion of a particular kind of social identity no less real than the demonstrable fact of social positions defined by relations of production. The history of a class is inseparable from the discursive claims about that class which seek to reorder the world in their own terms. Like a nation, a class is an 'imagined community' with its own sense of a common past, a common present, and a common destiny."
> —— `sources/monographs/siegelbaum_suny_1994_making_workers_soviet/ch03_class_backwards_in_search_of_the_soviet.md` [Book pp. 337–338 / PDF pp. 24, lines 321–327]

> "The research therefore is not to try to correlate class consciousness with interest, which itself is discursively constructed, or to see languages of class as simple reflections of underlying 'realities,' but to map out the landscape in which the fixing of identities (for example, the skilled male worker) privileged certain workers and suppressed alternative possibilities, and how such identities were reduced, expanded, challenged, and replaced in particular historical circumstances."
> —— `sources/monographs/siegelbaum_suny_1994_making_workers_soviet/ch03_class_backwards_in_search_of_the_soviet.md` [Book p. 338 / PDF p. 24, lines 337–343]
