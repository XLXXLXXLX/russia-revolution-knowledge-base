## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
埃德蒙森基于苏俄外交文件与美国ARA档案论证，苏维埃政权对1921年大饥荒的初期应对服从于“政治生存优先”而非人道与经济考量。面对农民起义、城市不满与西方借“粮食干涉”（1919年南森方案及匈牙利先例）颠覆政权的风险，莫斯科在1920年11月即获灾情预警，却先因强征种子恶化局面，继而刻意淡化灾情，并将新经济政策充作切断政权因果责任的政治“防疫隔离带”。1921年7月为博取民间外援而设的公共全俄赈饥委员会意外引来英法干涉派图谋，迫使当局一边设Pomgol并取缔该会，一边在8月秘密转向与ARA直接签约，最终“误打误撞”以迟滞救济导致的数百万死难换得千万灾民获救与政权脱险。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **早期预警与“隐瞒—拖延”的政治逻辑（pp. 506–509）**：伏尔加及辐射区涵盖约`3,750万`人口（最重12省）。`1920-11`《真理报》已报旱歉收，`12`月苏维埃八大却将“播种委员会”变成战时共产主义集中收种机构，加剧减种；`1921-01`与`06-10`（雷布尼科夫报告需`1亿`普特粮）预警再遭VTsIK调查组淡化，实因国家仅存粮种与铁路运力只够勉强保住城市根基，公开承认无力救灾会触发内外反对。NEP对饥民并无直接救济（伏尔加征粮更因行政混乱拖延至盛夏后），其真正作用是充当消除民众归罪政权的政治`cordon sanitaire` `[ch01–ch04]`。
2. **美国“粮食干涉”前史与两重赈饥委分立（pp. 509–515）**：1918–1919年胡佛、威尔逊推动以粮食遏制布尔什维主义，1919年4月南森方案要求接管交通与地方分发，1921年4月ARA官员T. T. C. Gregory更公开发文宣称以粮食瓦解了匈牙利苏维埃，使莫斯科极度戒备外援。1921年7月列宁听从高尔基斡旋，一方面于`07-21 [N.S.]`特许成立含库斯科娃、普罗科波维奇等非党名流共`63`人的**公共全俄赈饥委员会**以换取西方民间捐助（但私下称“从库斯科娃那里只取其名望与几车皮粮食，其余一概不给”），另一方面早在`07-18 [N.S.]`即设依附国家机关、10月末前未供一粒粮的政府**Pomgol**（加米涅夫主持）作为随时替代的政治备份 `[ch04–ch10]`。
3. **英法干涉企图与8月秘密ARA妥协（pp. 515–518）**：`1921-06-26`《真理报》与`07-13`高尔基呼吁暴露官方无力后，英法及协约国最高委员会（努朗斯照会）企图将公共赈饥委员会扶植为新联合政府并索要旧债与无监督调查权。苏俄遂于`08-12`经华沙鲁宾斯坦与加拉罕秘密联系美方，`08-18`消息抵美后24小时内达成ARA协议，随即清算公共委员会；至`1922-08`ARA供粮逾`1,000万`人 `[ch11–ch12]`。

## 原文核验引文（Verbatim Quotes）
> "The dilemma may be stated simply. If the Soviets launched a massive campaign, involving the solicitation of foreign help, to combat the human and economic aspects of the famine, they would be exposed to significant political risks. If the Soviets tried to minimize the danger of renewed political opposition in Russia and from abroad, they would be forced to ignore or 'underestimate' the famine and quietly accept serious loss of lives and economic damages.
> An examination of the Soviet response to the famine shows that the latter course was followed. Having chosen the strategy of political survival, the Soviets then committed tactical errors which nearly undermined the strategy. Further, the factor which extricated the regime from the famine, foreign relief, became available, and was accepted by the Soviets largely as a consequence of these errors." `[p. 507 / PDF p. 3 | sources/articles/edmondson_1977_politics_of_hunger_famine_1921/ch02_image_3.md#L13-L25]`

> "In its relationship to the famine, then, NEP could best be characterized as a political cordon sanitaire thoroughly compatible with the refusal to attempt conventional famine relief." `[p. 509 / PDF p. 5 | sources/articles/edmondson_1977_politics_of_hunger_famine_1921/ch04_image_5.md#L20-L22]`

> "As Lenin reassured a critic of this public committee: 'From Kuskova, we will get her name, her signature and a few carloads of food from people like her . . . and NOTHING [sic] more.'" `[p. 514 / PDF p. 10 | sources/articles/edmondson_1977_politics_of_hunger_famine_1921/ch09_image_10.md#L27-L30]`

> "The Soviets had blundered into an escape from the worst consequences of the famine. Lacking the resources and efficiency to defeat the menace of famine, the government had chosen to underestimate, and even ignore, its true size and seriousness. This policy reflected a willingness to absorb tremendous human and material losses in order to minimize political consequences. These losses would certainly have happened had not the government miscalculated the effects of creating the All-Russian Famine Relief Committee and then, to evade the consequences of miscalculation, accepted the assistance of the ARA." `[p. 518 / PDF p. 14 | sources/articles/edmondson_1977_politics_of_hunger_famine_1921/ch12_image_13.md#L84-L92]`
