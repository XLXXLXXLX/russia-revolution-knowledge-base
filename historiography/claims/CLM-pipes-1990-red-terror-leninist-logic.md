## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
派普斯认为，**红色恐怖并非内战或外国干涉逼出的临时自卫手段，而是布尔什维克政权自始嵌入的治理方式与列宁政治理念的逻辑延展**：与法国革命不同，俄革命“以恐怖开始”且恐怖从未退出体制；契卡在 1917 年 12 月、有组织反对尚未成形时已建立。列宁把恐怖视为弥补缺乏民众支持的**替补物（surrogate for popular support）**，并系统废除法治，以“革命良心”与契卡法外杀害取代 1864 年司法改革遗产。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **时序与制度化**：法俄恐怖对比——法恐怖为短促插曲，俄恐怖为政权 **essential element**；1920 年胜利后未 dismantle 恐怖机构。Cheka 1917 年 12 月成立，1918 上半年 Peters 承认未见有组织的反革命 `[ch. 18]`。
2. **列宁连续性**：1908 年批评公社“过度宽大”、应 **exterminate** 敌人；1917 年 11 月反对废除前线死刑；1919 年承认 imprison innocents 可取。意识形态上将阶级敌比喻为害虫 `[ch. 18]`。
3. **缺乏民意→恐怖加码**：“The more their popularity eroded, the more they resorted to terror.” 恐怖是 **weapon not of last resort** but **surrogate for popular support** `[ch. 18]`。
4. **废法国家**：专政定义 = **rule unrestricted by any law**；1917 年 11 月 22 日法令瓦解既存法院体系，革命法庭与契卡依 **revolutionary conscience** 行事；契卡权限事后被窜改文书扩大 `[ch. 18]`。
5. **Steinberg 见证**：恐怖是覆盖全民的 **system**，非偶发愤怒；列宁戏言司法人民委员部应名 **Commissariat for Social Extermination** `[ch. 18]`。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
> "Even so, the differences between Jacobin and Bolshevik practices in this respect are so profound that one can credit the Bolsheviks with having invented terror. Suffice it to say that the French Revolution culminated in terror, whereas the Russian one began with it." `[ch. 18]`

> "If terror had indeed been 'forced' on the Bolsheviks by 'foreign interventionists' and 'Russian reactionaries,' then they would have abandoned it as soon as they had decisively defeated these enemies—that is, in 1920. They did nothing of the kind." `[ch. 18]`

> "The evidence shows that Lenin, its most determined instigator, regarded terror as an indispensable instrument of revolutionary government. He was quite prepared to resort to it preventively—that is, in the absence of active opposition to his rule." `[ch. 18]`

> "how can you make a revolution without executions? Do you expect to dispose of your enemies by disarming yourself?" `[ch. 18, Lenin upon learning the Soviet congress had abolished the death penalty at the front, Nov. 1917]`

> "As it had been for the Jacobins, terror served the Bolsheviks not as a weapon of last resort, but as a surrogate for the popular support which eluded them." `[ch. 18]`

> "Terror is a system… a legalized plan of the regime for the purpose of mass intimidation, mass compulsion, mass extermination." `[ch. 18, quoting Isaac Steinberg, 1920]`

> "He defined the dictatorship of the proletariat as 'rule unrestricted by any law.'" `[ch. 18, citing Lenin, late 1918]`

> "Well put… that's exactly what it should be… but we can't say that." `[ch. 18, Lenin to Steinberg's jest about a 'Commissariat for Social Extermination', Feb. 1918]`
