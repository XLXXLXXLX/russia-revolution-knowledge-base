## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
利伯论证，1920 年代苏维埃工业化与 **乌克兰化（ukraïnizatsiia）/本土化** 构成共生进程：大规模农民向城市迁移（尤其在 1927—1929 一五前夕）逆转了帝俄以来城市俄罗斯化格局，至 1926–1929 年在乌克兰培育出一支规模迅速膨胀、身份日益乌克兰化的城市工人与迅速扩大的乌克兰语知识—官僚阶层，从而动摇俄罗斯中心多民族国家的政治整合前提。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **城市民族构成逆转**：1920 年代工业化改变乌克兰城市，“至 1933 年（甚至可能 1931 年）大多数城市居民已自我认同为乌克兰人”；此前城市为俄罗斯化熔炉，此转变对联盟政治统一影响重大 `[p. 75]`。
2. **速度超预期**：斯大林 1921 年十大曾援引里加四十年拉脱维亚化先例，但乌克兰多数大城市 **不到十年** 完成乌克兰化转折 `[pp. 99–100]`。
3. **工人阶级民族化**：1927 十五大后乌克兰投资上升，一五启动使 1928—1932 工人与官僚倍增，新增人口主要来自农村；乌克兰工人绝对数约 158.59 万（1926）增至 436.11 万（1932 年底），乌克兰人在工人中稳固多数；入城农民保留“农民性”，却与乌克兰化政策结合，强化乌克兰认同并加剧整合苏维埃秩序的难题 `[pp. 102–105]`。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
> "In the 1920s, the Soviet industrialisation drive transformed Ukraine's cities from Russian cities into Ukrainian cities. By 1933, and possibly as early as 1931, the majority of the urban population had come to identify itself as Ukrainian." `[p. 75]` *(English original, Liber 1992, as relayed in the repository Chinese edition.)*

> "It was clear that if the national republics' cities would in the course of time reflect the nationality composition of the surrounding countryside, then over time these cities would inevitably become Ukrainianised. Approximately forty years ago Riga was a German city, but with cities dependent on the countryside, and the countryside the bearer of the national element, Riga has become an almost purely Latvian city... Stalin supported this historically inevitable transformation of the national composition of cities and the proletariat, but neither he nor his colleagues were prepared for the speed at which their prognosis was fulfilled nor for its consequences. According to Stalin, Riga had taken forty years to Latvianise itself. Yet most Ukrainian cities became Ukrainianised in less than a decade." `[pp. 99–100]`

> "The massive rural-to-urban migration of Ukrainian peasants created conditions favourable to the cultural Ukrainianisation of the working class. Through korenizatsiia and trade-union cultural work, many workers who had earlier identified as Russians or whose identity had been ambiguous now identified as Ukrainians. As more Ukrainians entered the urban working class, their peasant alienation in the cities, combined with the government's Ukrainisation programme, reinforced their Ukrainianness—developments that in turn posed grave problems for their successful incorporation into the Soviet order." `[pp. 105–106]`

## 语境说明与局限提示
第三部十章及第二、四部对 1925—1929 教育、报刊与工会语言数据的展开（`ch10`，`pp. 155–160`）可作为本论断的行为文化侧证。利伯将 **语言使用** 与 **民族自我认同** 区分统计（1926 年工会普查显示认同乌克兰人比例高于日常说乌语比例，`ch08`），故不可把乌克兰化简单等同于 overnight linguistic homogeneity。本卡时间下限对齐任务至 1929 年底；1930 年后反乌克兰化逆转见同书第四部，不在此卡范围。
