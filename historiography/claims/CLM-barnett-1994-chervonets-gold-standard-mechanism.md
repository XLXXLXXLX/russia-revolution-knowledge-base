## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
文森特·巴尼特综合RGAE档案、苏联货币文献与定量准备数据指出，新经济政策时期的“切尔沃涅茨”并非按票面含金量自由兑现黄金的古典金本位货币，而是一种依赖央行储备干预维持目标汇率区间的受控纸币。尽管1922年11月4日人民委员会法令及切尔文票面文字均声称“将由政府特别法案确定兑金起始日”，但该法案从未出台。国家银行与财政部实际上借高达25%以上贵金属/外汇加商业票据的资产准备，以及在国内外交易所双向买卖黄金外汇的“类ERM”干预机制，在1922年底至1926年春将切尔文对英镑汇率大致稳在目标±20%以内；1925年海外大量流出引发的硬通货挤兑与1926年春储备剧减，最终迫使苏联在1926年3月终止自由外汇交易。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **制度辨伪：未兑现的“特别法案”承诺（pp. 663–665, 669）**：针对E. H. Carr（1958）关于莫斯科市场供求的模糊表述及Rostowski & Shapiro（1992）的不准确概括，作者核对`1922-11-04`人民委员会法令（`SU` no. 64, art. 827）及纸币票面（“3切尔文含纯金23.23克……兑换开始日期由特别法案规定”），并与A. Z. Arnold（1937）、L. N. Yurovsky（1925, 1928）、V. E. Manevich（1988）及索科利尼科夫1922年1月自辩相互印证，确证国家银行**从未**按金平价对公众直接兑换黄金（1924年春克拉辛提议固定汇率自由兑换外汇亦遭否决） `[ch00#L67-L121, #L171-L208, #L447-L476]`。
2. **汇率干预机制与1925–1926年外流危机（pp. 664–666, 669）**：借用1925年A. A. 索科洛夫的理论区分，切尔文之稳定源自国家银行及1923年2月设立的特别外汇委员会实施汇率干预（`1922-12`至`1924-02`净卖出`98万英镑`与`460万美元`；莫斯科自由市场对英镑在1923-01至1926-04间波动约±20%）。`RGAE f. 7733, op. 2, del. 275`（`1925-07-09`卡岑涅连鲍姆致尤罗夫斯基信）揭示，1925年上半年仅里加一地即流入约`115,000`切尔文、柏林亦积聚大量切尔文，耗竭外汇护盘能力，导致`1926-03`行政叫停私人自由外汇黄金买卖 `[ch00#L122-L166, #L214-L223, #L420-L441]`。
3. **资产准备与储备消长的定量佐证（Tables 1–3, pp. 666–668）**：`1922-11`至`1925-01`发行的资产准备总额从`514.6千`升至`59,750.0千`切尔文（黄金占比由`79.0%`降至`23.7%`，商业票据由`0`升至`48.8%`）；流通量对储备拨付额比从`1923-01-01`的`32%`（`35.6万/111.8万`）升至`1924-03-01`的`92%`（`3,002.7万/3,280万`）；而行情研究所数据显示国家银行总储备于`1926-01`至`04`由`3.193亿`（金`1.824亿`）骤跌至`2.65亿`卢布（金`1.475亿`），印证了1926年春放弃汇率干预的物质约束 `[ch00#L225-L373]`。

## 原文核验引文（Verbatim Quotes）
> "Sokolov's argument shows that direct convertibility into gold at par for a specific currency is not the same as a currency being supported at a certain rate in relation to gold and foreign currency by the relevant monetary authorities. In the former case a promise has been given to convert all notes at a certain rate no matter when they are presented for exchange, whereas in the latter a promise has been given to try to maintain a certain rate through currency intervention, but no guarantees of maintaining this rate are given." `[p. 665 / PDF p. 4 | sources/articles/barnett_1994_as_good_as_gold_chervonets/ch00_pages_1-8.md#L156-L162]`

> "Thus the chervonets was never directly convertible into gold at par, rather Gosbank purchased chervontsy for gold and foreign currency as a mechanism for supporting the exchange rate at a certain level. If Manevich is right than a mild deception had been carried out by the Soviet authorities. A promise to convert chervontsy at par some time in the future was given but never kept, and this technique proved successful (together with currency intervention) in introducing and maintaining a stable rate of exchange for the new currency." `[pp. 665–666 / PDF pp. 4–5 | sources/articles/barnett_1994_as_good_as_gold_chervonets/ch00_pages_1-8.md#L176-L188]`

> "This note has suggested the importance of distinguishing between a currency exchangeable for gold at par and a currency which is supported at a certain rate by central bank operations. It seems that the chervonets was the latter but not the former. It was also suggested that the actual level of gold and foreign exchange cover which existed for the chervonets was quite high, although a definitive answer on this question will not be possible until full access is given to the relevant archives in Russia." `[p. 668 / PDF p. 7 | sources/articles/barnett_1994_as_good_as_gold_chervonets/ch00_pages_1-8.md#L375-L381]`
