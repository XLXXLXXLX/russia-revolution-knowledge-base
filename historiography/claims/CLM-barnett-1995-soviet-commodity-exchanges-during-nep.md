## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
文森特·巴尼特运用一阶差分相关性与季节调整方法，对1923—1929年苏联私商及集市商品价格序列进行了计量检验，并与同期美国市场及战前沙俄农产市场对标，借此重估“NEP混合经济替代斯大林计划工业化”的可行性。巴尼特证明：虽然1920年代中期私商与农村集市在社会零售额中占有半壁江山、莫斯科与70座外省交易所亦恢复运转，但大宗商品交易所实由国营机构（占买方五至六成、卖方八至九成）主导“做市”；经一阶差分剔除通胀与自相关后，全苏商品市场整合度整体偏低，仅肉类、部分毗邻省区谷物与莫斯科腹地燕麦圈形成局部统一市场，远逊于1914年前帝国统一粮市与美国同类市场。故若1929年后沿NEP推进工业化，仍需大量补建市场基础设施与商业文化。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **方法论纠偏与双重基准（pp. 329–338）**：作者指出单看地区价格极差（如Ozerov 1926所用价格振幅）不足以否定统一市场，但高水平相关性又常被通胀趋势和自相关虚高（如A3砂糖水平值`0.980–0.997`实系通胀假象）。故须以**一阶差分**相关系数检验价格波动传导，并以美国1926–1927年批发市场（黄油`0.963–0.985`、马铃薯`0.434–0.802`、石油`0.273–0.403`）及Koval'chenko与Milov测算的19世纪末沙俄49省统一黑麦市场（`r > 0.70`）作标尺 `[ch01–ch09, ch17]`。
2. **私商规模与大宗交易所的“国营做市者”本质（pp. 335–337, 342–345）**：行情研究所数据显示，`1924–25`与`1925–26`私商仅占总流转额`23.3%`与`19.6%`（但占全国商业网点`84.1%–77.1%`，因多系微小零售与集市摊贩，1924–1926上半年颁照`49.6万`增至`60.8万`张）；谷物受运费约束强烈（莫斯科谷物`83.9%`运距<477公里），而`1924/25`工业品交易所成交`68.2%`集中于莫斯科。关键在Table 9：在莫斯科商品交易所（MTB）与70座外省交易所，国营机关分别占买方**`66.8%`/`51.7%`**、卖方**`90.6%`/`83.4%`**，而私人买/卖仅占**`6.2%`/`3.7%`**与**`10.1%`（原OCR误印MI.1）/`4.9%`**，表明交易所远非纯自由竞争市场 `[ch07–ch08, ch14–ch16]`。
3. **跨区域价格整合度检验与NEP替代辩论（pp. 338–346, 347–351）**：A1–A6矩阵显示：一阶差分后，食物市场（尤其肉类，如RSFSR消费区—白俄`0.860`、乌克兰—高加索`0.817`）显著比国营调控更强的金属、制造业整合；谷物仅在地理邻近区呈现高整合（如1925–1926莫斯科—乌拉尔大麦`0.977`、白俄—中央农业区黑麦`0.967`、白俄—乌克兰黑麦`0.942`、莫斯科周边六县市燕麦`0.780–0.973`），而跨大区（尤其中亚、西伯利亚与欧俄间）整体关联度偏低，从而为延续NEP替代方案增添了市场整合不足的制度难度 `[ch10–ch13, ch17–ch23]`。

## 原文核验引文（Verbatim Quotes）
> "This investigation relates to what is usually taken to be the 'big issue' of NEP in the existing historiographical literature-the viability of a mixed economy alternative to Stalin's centrally planned industrialization policy. If no level of market integration is detected in the actual NEP economy, then an industrialization drive based on a mixed economy would have required much development of market infrastructure and culture. If, on the other hand, NEP markets were reasonably integrated, then some basic level of all-Union market infrastructure could be said to exist. This would have aided industrialization within the NEP framework." `[p. 330 / PDF p. 3 | sources/articles/barnett_1995_soviet_commodity_markets_nep/ch02_p_331.md#L33-L43]`

> "The question of whether a state organ can be the major player in 'free markets' without transforming these into regulated markets applies to such institutions as the MTB, as coordinated action by these state organs is likely to turn them into market makers rather than market takers." `[p. 343 / PDF p. 16 | sources/articles/barnett_1995_soviet_commodity_markets_nep/ch15_p_344.md#L43-L47]`

> "From the datasets analysed in section V it is possible to draw the tentative conclusion that there was at least some degree of integration of Soviet commodity markets during NEP, but that the level differed for different goods and for different regions. However, only a small number of highly integrated markets (first difference correlation greater than o.8o) were detected, and thus 'free markets' could have existed in only a small number of cases (using the argument given at the beginning of this article).
> Comparison with equivalent US data reveals that Soviet integration was generally not lower than its US counterpart in only a few cases, and indications of the quantities traded on 'free markets' (private trade and peasant bazaars) shows that such markets were an important part of the NEP economy. In relation to the question of the viability of the continuation of NEP as an alternative to Stalinist industrialization strategy, these results imply that NEP markets were not generally highly integrated, and hence that any industrialization strategy based on a mixed economy which may have been desirable after I929 would have required much development of market culture. This does not make the task impossible, but adds requirements which complicate the matter further." `[p. 345 / PDF p. 18 | sources/articles/barnett_1995_soviet_commodity_markets_nep/ch17_p_346.md#L11-L28]` *(注：原OCR切片第15、26行将 `0.80`、`1929` 分别转写为 `o.8o`、`I929`，此处逐字保留原切片文本。)*

> "The actual extent of integrated markets which could be said to exist from the analysis carried out in this article may make future writers on NEP more cautious about applying the description 'free market' to this period. Lenin's enthusiastic statements about allowing the rebirth of 'market forces' may be seen as a little exaggerated, at least as regards their geographical extent." `[p. 346 / PDF p. 19 | sources/articles/barnett_1995_soviet_commodity_markets_nep/ch18_p_347.md#L35-L40]`
