## 核心论断命题（Thesis）

约瑟夫·戈德森（Joseph Godson）从列宁主义工会教义与组织结构出发指出，一旦共产党夺取政权，“工会作为保护成员福祉、警惕国家或资方家长制（state or management paternalism）的独立机构”这一概念便遭到根本颠覆：基于“工人阶级已掌权”的教条，任何针对国家或厂方的抗议与罢工在逻辑上均被宣布为“工人自己反对自己”从而不可容忍。列宁自 1920–1921 年工会大辩论（俄共十大前夕《列宁全集》第 42 卷第 203 页）起将工会定型为“**管理的学校、共产主义的学校**”及党通向群众的“**传送带（transmission belts）**”，并依托“**民主集中制**＋**全厂单一产业工会原则**＋**秘密党组内部操纵**”锁死底层独立空间；在组织人事上，**1929 年全苏工会中央理事会（AUCCTU）主席米哈伊尔·托姆斯基被罢免**标志着真正拥有工会工作资历的领导层彻底终结，此后工会主席一律由党务行政官员委派，而 1920 年代尚有一定实际效力的劳资集体合同亦在随后的五年计划中被摧毁。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）

1. **夺权前后的教义大反转**：夺权前，共产党人在资本主义工会内竭力煽动对国家体制的敌意；夺权后，基于“生产资料已归工人国家所有”的假定，罢工或停工被斥为有害且不合逻辑，工会几乎彻底交出作为独立利益捍卫者的地位，转而服从工业生产增长与国家体制维持 `[pp. 106–107]` `[PDF pp. 118–119]`。
2. **俄共十大辩论谱系中的列宁定义（“共产主义学校”与“传送带”）**：戈德森援引《列宁全集》第 42 卷第 203 页（注 4，1920 年 12 月–1921 年 1 月工会争论核心文献）指出，列宁将工会定义为“管理的学校、经济管理的学校、共产主义的学校”以及“从共产党通向群众的传送带”；苏联官方理论家（如 Portnoi & Fedyukov 1972；Sharapov 1973）据此长期坚持工会形式上非党、实质上在党直接领导下贯彻党代会任务 `[pp. 107–108, 128]` `[PDF pp. 119–120, 140]`。
3. **双重结构钳制（民主集中制＋产业原则＋秘密党组）**：苏联工会建基于两大组织原则——（1）“民主集中制”，即下级必须服从上级，且工会每一级机构内凡有 3 名以上党员即组成**秘密党组（Party groups）**，严格服从同级党委（全苏工会中央理事会党员受制于苏共中央书记处主管部）；（2）“产业原则（production principle）”，强制同一企业所有职工归属同一工会，禁止另立工会 `[pp. 109–111]` `[PDF pp. 121–123]`。
4. **1929 年托姆斯基被免职的分水岭与 1920 年代集体合同余晖**：戈德森指出，除 **1929 年被解职的米哈伊尔·托姆斯基（Mikhail Tomsky）** 外，AUCCTU 主席一贯是缺乏工会资历、按党意委派的党务官员（如什维尔尼克等，后延及前克格勃首脑谢列平）`[pp. 109–110]` `[PDF pp. 121–122]`；此外，援引征服者（Robert Conquest）与多伊彻（Isaac Deutscher, 1950），指出“**在 1920 年代尚具某种现实意义的工会与雇主之间的集体合同**”成为五年计划的牺牲品 `[p. 121, 129]` `[PDF pp. 133, 141]`。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）

> "The communist view, as enunciated by Lenin and his successors, ofthe [of the] nature of trade unions and their relation to the state changes radically once a communist regime comes into power. Prior to a take-over, communists inside the trade union movement strive unceasingly and by all means available to generate hostility to the capitalist state. Once in power, with the state now supposedly on the side of the workers, the relationship is totally changed. This apparently signifies the trade unions' alm ost [almost] total surrender of their position as independent institutions to promote and defend the workers' interests and welfare." `[p. 106]` `[PDF p. 118]`

> "In theory, then, since the trade unions represent the working dass [class], and since the working dass [class] is said to hold state power and owns the means of production and means of exchange, any trade union criticism of, or opposition, to the state or management would be tantamount to the working dass [class] fighting against itself. Unions in a comm unist [communist] state are thus asked to view any type of disruptive industrial action such as strikes or work stoppages not only as harmful to the national interest, but also as totally illogical and therefore intolerable." `[p. 107]` `[PDF p. 119]`

> "The definition of a trade union most frequently cited in communist countries in this connection is the one by Lenin that it is 'a school of administration, a school of economic management, a school of communism'.4" `[p. 107]` `[PDF p. 119]`
*(原书注 4 `[p. 128]` `[PDF p. 140]`：`Complete Collected Works, vol. 42, p. 203.`)*

> "Thus I. L. Portnoi and N. Y. Fedyukov, in an attempt to refute 'bourgeois myths' about the relationship of Soviet trade unions and the Communist Party of the Soviet Union (CPSU), quoted Lenin's classic description of the trade unions as 'the transmission belts from the Communist Party to the masses' and described party leadership as 'a guarantee of success in the work of our unions'.5" `[p. 108]` `[PDF p. 120]`

> "The bases upon which Soviet trade union structure is built are: (I) [(1)] The principle of democratic centralism which theoretically means that all union organs from bottom to top are elected by union members and are accountable to them and that the lower organs of a union are subordinate to higher ones. The operative principle here is that which binds lower organs to accept the decisions of higher organs and the fact that all union activities are controlled by the party from within, through party groups within all trade union bodies and meetings as weil [well] as through the recommendation or appointment of party members to responsible positions. (2) The production principle, which means that all persons working in the same enterprise belong to the same union. In practice, this machinery has afforded neither independence nor democracy." `[p. 109]` `[PDF p. 121]`

> "The President ofthe [of the] AUCCTU has always been a party man, with little or no experience oftrade [of trade] union work (except for Mikhail Tomsky, who was dismissed in 1929) and appointed at the will ofthe [of the] party." `[pp. 109–110]` `[PDF pp. 121–122]`

> "Collective agreements between trade unions and employers, which had so me [some] reality in the 1920s, were a victim of the five-year plans and ceased to be concluded from 1937 until 1947.23" `[p. 121]` `[PDF p. 133]`
