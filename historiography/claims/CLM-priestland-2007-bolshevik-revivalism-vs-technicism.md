## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
普里斯特兰认为，战间布尔什维克精英话语可在马克思主义目的论框架下划分为五种相互张力、可临时组合的立场；其中对 1920 年代路线争论最具解释力的是非 **复兴主义**（精英/民粹）与 **技术主义**（含自由技术主义）的对立，而非“国家 vs 社会”或“斯大林主义 vs 真马克思主义”的冲突论二分。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **技术主义的界定**：技术主义者（1920 年代常斥为“右派”、关联布哈林）视工人具有利己理性的行为主体，依赖物质奖励与纪律，强调“经济/技术”优先于“政治”，怀疑唯意志主义跃进，相对淡化意识形态统一与阶级斗争策略 `[pp. 54–55]`。
2. **复兴主义的动员逻辑**：复兴主义正当化群众热情以弥补基建与教育薄弱，要求“政治/意识形态”优先，但日益损害技术专家地位，与理性计划经济难以兼容；民粹复兴主义更易激化分裂 `[pp. 59–60]`。
3. **NEP 时期的组合**：1921–1927 领导层务实地“退却”，组合技术主义与布哈林式自由技术主义；民粹/精英复兴主义者结盟反对，要求“阶级斗争”与向社会主义“前进”，为 1927–1929 左转埋设语义资源 `[pp. 67–68]`。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
> "Technicism is a form of Bolshevism that was often condemned as 'right wing', especially in the 1920s in connection with Bukharin and his group. Like elite revivalists, technicists were opposed to populist revivalist claims that the working class was already mature; they were also sceptical that workers and officials could be inspired to achieve extraordinary feats if given the right incentives. Following a scientistic Marxism, they viewed workers as selfish, rational actors, believing that the most effective incentives for them were material rewards and discipline rather than enthusiasm or some distinctive proletarian consciousness. More broadly, they were dissatisfied with voluntarist tendencies among elite revivalists. They denied that political will could drive society towards communism and argued that the leadership must base its policies on scientific requirements. In the language of the time, technē had to be given priority over politics (politika)." `[p. 54]`

> "Populist and elite revivalists found common ground during the NEP period in an alliance opposed to technicism and liberal technicism, supporting 'class struggle' and a march 'forwards' to socialism. Influential forms of revivalism that would have a major influence in the 1930s emerged in the latter half of the 1920s." `[p. 68]`
