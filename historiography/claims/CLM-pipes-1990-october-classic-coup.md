## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
派普斯主张，1917 年 10 月 25–26 日（O.S.）事件在性质上是**经精心策划的现代政变（coup d’état）**，而非苏维埃民主进程的正当延续或群众社会革命的自发高潮。布尔什维克通过篡改苏维埃代表规则、控制军事革命委员会（Milrevkom）、在苏韦埃代表大会开幕前以小型突击部队占领通讯与军事枢纽完成夺权，并以“双重政权”惯例伪装合法性；当时绝大多数民众并不理解政权已发生根本性断裂。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **篡夺苏维埃全国机器**：北方区域委员会召集的“第二届全俄苏维埃代表大会”违反执行委员会规程，是夺权的第一阶段“veritable coup d’état against the national organization of the soviets” `[ch. 11]`。
2. **Milrevkom 工具化**：Petrograd Soviet 的城防委员会实为布尔什维克军事部的“flag of convenience”；Central Committee 10 月 10 日决定武装起事，列宁要求抢在代表大会前夺权，代表大会仅可作掩护 `[ch. 11]`。
3. **Malaparte 式无声政变**：放弃大规模街头示威，改由纪律部队占领都市“神经中枢”；行动进行时市内娱乐场所照常开放，亲历者亦强调几乎无街头战斗感知 `[ch. 11]`。
4. **民意与欺骗**：卫戍部队多数中立，布尔什维克只需阻止政府动员军队；托洛茨基承认利用 Kerensky 时代的 dual power 惯例掩盖卫戍起义。革命后一周官方文件刻意回避 “socialism” 以安抚人心；大众对换政府漠不关心 `[ch. 11]`。
5. **总括语（Introduction）**：1905–1917 序列“culminating in the Bolshevik coup d’etat of October” `[Introduction]`。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
> "culminating in the Bolshevik coup d'etat of October." `[Introduction]`

> "This Bolshevik move constituted a veritable coup d'état against the national organization of the soviets: it was the opening phase of the power seizure." `[ch. 11]`

> "With supreme mastery of the technique of the modern coup d'état, of which he was arguably the inventor, he led the Bolsheviks to victory." `[ch. 11]`

> "the question of the Second Congress of Soviets… held for him no interest whatever: of what importance is it?… It is necessary to tear out [vyrazi'] power. One must not tie oneself to the Congress of Soviets, it is silly and absurd to forewarn the enemy about the date of the uprising." `[ch. 11, Lenin at Oct. 10 CC meeting, per Trotsky recollection]`

> "The characteristic quality of modern revolutions is precisely the bloodless, almost silent seizure of strategic points by small detachments of trained shock troops.… This description fits the October coup in Russia (which Malaparte had studied and used as one of his models)." `[ch. 11]`

> "That night (October 24–25), the Bolsheviks systematically took over all the objectives of strategic importance… Thus, under cover of darkness, one by one, railroad stations, post offices, telephone centers, banks, and bridges fell under Bolshevik control. No resistance was encountered, no shots fired.… it was a model modern coup d'état as described by Malaparte." `[ch. 11]`

> "The vast majority of Russia's inhabitants at the time had no inkling of what had happened.… The result of the deception was that the total break in government went virtually unnoticed, appearing merely as a 'legal' resolution of yet another governmental crisis." `[ch. 11]`

## 语境说明与局限提示
本章详述 Kornilov 事件铺垫与 Kerensky 误判，但政变定性不依赖于科尔尼洛夫阴谋真伪，而强调**政党阴谋—军事夺点—苏维埃合法化**三联。与 Rabinowitch、Smith 等强调地方苏维埃民主动力、群众参与的修订派解释尖锐对立，宜在 `DEB-1917-october-coup-or-social-revolution` 下对照 MILREV 档案与代表资格统计。派普斯引托洛茨基、斯大林事后自白证明“进攻伪装防御”，须区分宣传陈述与行动日志。
