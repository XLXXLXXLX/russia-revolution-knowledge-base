## 核心论断命题（Thesis）

邦内尔认为，在帝俄晚期金属加工业中，熟练工人阶层通过对 `masterovoi` 与 `rabochii` 名分、同业仪表与技艺阶梯的极度敏感，在车间内部建构了一套**前党派化的尊严秩序**；“觉悟工人”（soznatel'nye rabochie）则在此基础上叠加城市教养、识字与社会主义化话语，使车间等级与政治激进化相互强化——卡纳奇科夫从学徒到样板工、再向往彼得堡“觉悟”共同体的人生轨迹，即是这一双重过程的典型微观史个案。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）

1. **名分即尊严**：熟练金属工将统称 `rabochii` 视为侮辱，管理层抹杀 `masterovoi` 类别可触发罢工；社会互动与工头礼数随工人着装而变（`ch01` 导论 `pp. 12–13`；蒂莫费耶夫 `ch04` `pp. 75–76`）。
2. **车间技艺贵族**：卡纳奇科夫描写样板工车间为“贵族”作坊——体面着装、自重举止、嘲笑“灰魔鬼”村匠型同工；晋升样板工伴随工资作为“社会有用性”的道德估价（`ch02` `pp. 46–48, 55–56`）。
3. **政治化的“觉悟”层**：导论将卡纳奇科夫界定为从农民经“conscious worker”至政治活动家的道路；其赴彼得堡动机是想象中多数工人已“觉悟”并互助反抗工头与业主（`ch01` `p. 31`；`ch02` `pp. 71, 1123–1128`）。

> "Workers attached enormous importance to these designations and were bitterly opposed when factory management sought to alter them by eliminating the category of masterovoi, reclassifying skilled groups as rabochie, and applying the term chemorabochie to the remainder. One such episode is discussed at some length by Timofeev, but the workers' preoccupation with matters of status differentiation emerges from all of the selections below."
> —— `sources/primary/bonnell_1983_russian_worker_memoirs/ch01_pages_31-60.md` [Book p. 12 / PDF p. 31]

> "The pattern shop was considered to be the 'aristocratic' workshop. Most of the patternmakers were urban types—they dressed neatly, wore their trousers over their boots, wore their shirts 'fantasia' style, tucked into their trousers, fastened their collars with a colored lace instead of a necktie, and on holidays some of them even wore bowler hats. … Their bearing was firm, conveying their consciousness of their own worth."
> —— `sources/primary/bonnell_1983_russian_worker_memoirs/ch02_pages_61-90.md` [Book p. 46 / PDF p. 64]

> "At this point he began the long journey from peasant to 'conscious' worker and political activist."
> —— `sources/primary/bonnell_1983_russian_worker_memoirs/ch01_pages_31-60.md` [Book p. 31 / PDF p. 49]

> "I pictured that city as 'the promised land,' where the majority of workers were 'conscious' and lived a cordial, comradely life, supporting one another in their struggle against foremen and owners."
