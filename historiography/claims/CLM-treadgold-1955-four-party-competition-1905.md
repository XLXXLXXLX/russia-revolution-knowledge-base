## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
特雷德戈尔德认为，1905 年革命爆发时，俄国四大政治领袖——切尔诺夫（社革党）、列宁（布尔什维克）、马尔托夫（孟什维克）、米留可夫（立宪民主党）——已对即将发生的变革持有**互斥而又部分重叠**的革命类型学：皆否认即刻的社会主义革命，却分别将其命名为“劳动者革命”“资产阶级民主革命”“资产阶级革命”与（策略性接受的）资产阶级立宪革命。这些命名预先规定了联盟对象、临时政府公式及对自由派的态度，从而在 1905 年“大考”中构成零和式策略竞争。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **共识与命名分裂**：血腥星期日后，各反对派领袖确信知 revolution 之性质；一致预期将终结专制、确立民主自由与社会改革，但在如何称呼革命上分道 `[pp. 138–139, Part Three opening]`。
2. **马尔托夫路线**：将进程视为“资产阶级革命”，工人应保持政治领导却推动自由派完成摧毁专制；拒绝社会民主党人进入资产阶级临时政府，相信历史将强制资产阶级掌权 `[pp. 179–192, ch14]`。
3. **列宁路线**：《两种策略》主张工农革命民主专政，预先裁定自由派必将叛卖；结局取决于工人是作自由派 powerful 附庸还是人民革命领袖 `[pp. 170–171, 179–183, ch14]`。
4. **切尔诺夫路线**：拒“资产阶级”标签，主张劳动者政治革命可短间隔衔接社会革命，与列宁共拒中产阶级霸权，与孟什维克在自由派角色上分歧 `[pp. 138–142, 151–153, ch13]`。
5. **结论章综合**（1906 视角）：三大党在杜马出现前即按 peasants/workers/middle class 三分建构政党；1905 考验了谁更能把理论预言转化为可动员的战术语言 `[pp. 255–261, 266–273, ch17]`。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
> "When the revolutionary storm broke at last with the massacre of Bloody Sunday, all the opposition leaders were convinced that they knew what sort of upheaval was in store for Russia. ... They all knew it was not to be a 'socialist' revolution. They all knew it would eliminate the absolute power of the Tsar, and transfer the power to a democracy, that it would inaugurate an era of personal and public freedom, and that it would end in the enactment of sweeping social reforms. Martov called it a 'bourgeois' revolution; Miliukov accepted the term though would not use it himself; Lenin insisted on calling it a 'bourgeois democratic' one; Chernov refused to accept or use the word 'bourgeois' at all, and spoke of a 'toilers'' (trudovaia) or 'political' revolution." `[pp. 138–139]`

> "In other words, we may interpret Lenin as saying that if the emphasis is legal, the worker will play second fiddle; if illegal, he will lead." `[p. 170]`

> "Russia was on the threshold of bourgeois democracy. ... The Russian proletariat was more advanced by far than the Western proletariat had been at the time of the classic bourgeois revolutions. Therefore the workers' role in 1905 was crucial, central, and leading, but not to be played over the footlights, Miliukov was to play Trilby to Martov's Svengali." `[pp. 191–192]`
