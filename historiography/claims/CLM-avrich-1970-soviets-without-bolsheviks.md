## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
Avrich 指出，喀琅施塔特 1921 年政治纲领的核心是 **重建不受政党操控的自由苏维埃**（口号「一切权力归苏维埃，而非政党」），要求为劳动者、无政府派与左翼社会主义者开放言论与选举权，但 **并未** 主张「苏维埃不要共产党人」（Soviets without Communists）——后者是内战期间西伯利亚农民与马赫诺派的标语；流亡自由派米留科夫在巴黎的概括（«Soviets instead of Bolsheviks»、排除共产党后再谈立宪会议）歪曲了叛军立场。叛军允许布尔什维克在苏维埃中与孟什维克、社会革命党、无政府派并存，且内部仍有大量党员参与；其目标是 **改革并去垄断化** 苏维埃与共产主义，而非在法律上取缔共产党。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **纲领正面陈述**：Petropavlovsk 决议第 1–2 点要求秘密选举苏维埃，并为「workers and peasants, anarchists and left socialist parties」保障言论出版自由 `[pp. 86-87, ch.2]`；ch.5 概括运动时强调 **反对任何单一政党独占**，要打破共产党垄断 `[pp. 178-181]`。
2. **报头口号**：《伊兹维斯蒂亚》刊「All power to the soviets but not the parties»；无线电 3 月 6 日广播坚持「freely elected representation of the toilers» `[pp. 179-180]`。
3. **阶级性民主边界**：自由仅及于劳动者，非地主资产阶级；仍反对立宪会议，要直接苏维埃民选 `[pp. 179-180, 162-163]`。
4. **纠偏「苏维埃不要共产党人」**：Avrich 专节：该 slogan **从未** 为喀琅施塔特采用；出自西伯利亚与马赫诺；米留科夫 «Poslednie Novosti» 将其渲染为驱逐共产党后的多党苏维埃并通向立宪会议，与叛军文本冲突 `[pp. 198-201]`。
5. **实践区分**：起义期间 **revtroiki** 曾排斥共产党员，但 **代表会议** 中共产党人仍占显著比例（约三分之一）；叛军无意消灭党，而要 **净化** 其官僚化、恢复革命初旨 `[pp. 198-201, 914-937]`。
6. **与党内反对派对比**：「舰队反对派」、工人反对派等仍维护布尔什维克 **执政垄断**，喀琅施塔特则要求 **分享苏维埃权力** 给左翼反对党 `[pp. 199, 939-949]`。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
> "For all their animosity towards the Bolshevik hierarchy, the sailors never called for the disbandment of the party or its exclusion from a role in Russian government and society. 'Soviets without Communists' was not, as is often maintained by both Soviet and non-Soviet writers, a Kronstadt slogan. Such a slogan did exist: it had been trumpeted by peasant bands in Siberia during the Civil War, and Makhno's partisans in the south had similarly declared themselves 'For the soviets but against the Communists.' But the sailors never appropriated these watchwords." `[pp. 198-199]`

> "The sailors, he wrote, wanted power to pass from the existing one-party dictatorship to a coalition of socialists and nonparty radicals, acting through soviets from which the Communists had been banished. Such an arrangement, he said, would leave ample room for a restoration of the Constituent Assembly on the national level. This, however, was a far from accurate description of the Kronstadt program, which explicitly rejected the Constituent Assembly and which did allow a place for the Bolsheviks in the soviets, alongside the other left-wing political organizations." `[p. 201]`

> "'All power to the soviets but not the parties.' 'Our cause is just,' declared the Petropavlovsk radio on March 6. 'We stand for power to the soviets but not the parties, for the freely elected representation of the toilers. The soviets that have been captured and manipulated by the Communist party have always been deaf to all our demands and needs; the only reply we have ever received has been shooting.'" `[pp. 179-180]`

## 语境说明与局限提示
论断选自第五章意识形态总论，与 Petropavlovsk 十五点及 rebel *Izvestiia* 社论（附录 B、C）互证。Avrich 区分 **口号学** 与 **实际权力安排**（临时排除共产党员于自卫隊 vs 允许多党苏维埃选举）。苏联史学与部分流亡报刊将运动简化为「反共」，Avrich 旨在将其重置于 **1917 苏维埃民主诉求** 与 **战时共产主义抵抗** 光谱。读者应注意叛军 **反立宪会议、反资产阶级自由** 的激进左翼属性，避免将其误读为自由民主复辟。
