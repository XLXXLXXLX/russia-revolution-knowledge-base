## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
针对托洛茨基及苏联宣传所称「1921 年喀琅施塔特水兵已非 1917 年革命水手，而是由对苏维埃冷漠甚至敌视的农户新兵所取代」，Avrich 承认内战期间波罗的海舰队人员换血与农民成分上升（1921 年逾 75% 为农民出身），但论证 **舰队行为模式未发生根本蜕变**：农村青年海员历来便是喀琅施塔特激进主义的温床；「阶级蜕化」论调在 1918 年兵变后已被用作卸责工具；而 **1921 年起义由服役多年的资深水兵与战列舰技师领衔**；新老水兵在反对征粮与中央集权上立场相近。故托洛茨基式「替换了老水兵」说 **不能解释** 起义的性质与领导力，而只是政权为难以驾驭的持续异议提供的 **解释性借口**。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **官方叙事复述**：托洛茨基称老将伤亡调散，舰队由乌克兰等地农民新兵填充，带无政府—匪帮心态，有的在白军服役过 `[pp. 101-102]`。
2. **统计事实**：官方数据 1921 年四分之三海员农民出身，高于 1917；佩特里琴科承认许多战友为南俄农民 `[pp. 102-103]`。
3. **连续性论证**：舰队中一直有大批 unruly 农村成分；1905、1917 暴动即由其推动；整个内战期间喀琅施塔特人仍难控制、屡遭调离，`malcontents` 被发往偏远岗位 `[pp. 103]`。
4. **领导层结构**：起义时 **老海员**（有的战前入伍）居主导；佩特里琴科 1912 年入伍；雅科文科 1917 年参战；领导层多为战列舰 **高级办事员、技师**；15 人临委会中 9 人为水手、4 工员、2 白领， **无可争辩的 plebeian** 领导 `[pp. 103-106]`。
5. **反蜕化叙事**：1920 年秋共产党仍将水兵树为革命英雄，无人谈「阶级蜕化」；该指控在 1918 年 10 月彼得格勒海军站兵变后即已出现，彼时成分尚无巨变 `[pp. 104-105]`。
6. **阶层内部同质性**：「老油条」与「新兵」均多农民出身，亲历农村苦难，共同反对强制政府；起义爆发时 **`the older seamen … took the lead`** `[pp. 106-114]`。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
> "As portrayed by the Bolsheviks, then, the Kronstadt seaman of 1921 was 'of a different social and psychological makeup' from his predecessor of the Revolution and Civil War: at worst, a corrupt and demoralized roughneck, undisciplined, foul-mouthed, and given to card playing and drink; at best, 'a peasant lad in a sailor suit,' a simple country bumpkin sporting bell-bottom trousers and a heavily pomaded pompadour to attract female admirers." `[p. 102]`

> "Yet this does not necessarily mean that the behavioral patterns of the fleet had undergone any fundamental change. On the contrary, alongside the technical ratings, who were drawn largely from the working class, there had always been a large and unruly peasant element among the sailors, an element lacking in discipline and prone to run amok at the least provocation. Indeed, in 1905 and 1917 it was these very youths from the countryside who had given Kronstadt its reputation as a hotbed of revolutionary extremism." `[p. 103]`

> "Actually, there was little to distinguish the old-timers from the recent recruits in their midst. Both groups were largely of peasant background; both—the one while on furlough, the other before reporting for active duty—had seen for themselves the misery in their native districts; and both longed to cast off the coercive authority of the central government. Not unexpectedly, when the rebellion finally erupted, it was the older seamen, veterans of many years of service (dating in some cases from before the First World War) who took the lead." `[pp. 106-107]`

> "The allegation that politically retarded muzhiks had diluted the revolutionary character of the fleet, it would seem, was largely a device to explain away dissident movements among the sailors, and had been used as such as early as October 1918, following the abortive mutiny at the Petrograd naval station, when the social composition of the fleet could not yet have undergone any sweeping transformation." `[pp. 104-105]`

## 语境说明与局限提示
本章开篇先完整呈现布尔什维克—托洛茨基立场，再转入档案化的人口学与舰上政治社会史分析；脚注援引 Pukhov、Kornatovsky、Katkov、托洛茨基 1938 年文集等。Avrich 未否定成分变迁 **量** 变，而否定其 **质** 变及因果论断；对乌克兰—反犹—民族因素的讨论见同章后文（`[pp. 105-108]`），本论断卡聚焦于 **社会阶层与「老/新水兵」神话**。量化同期对比仍依赖苏联 1930 年代出版物，终审有待舰队人事档案。
