## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
罗斯洛夫依托GARF、RTsKhIDNI等六座中央与地方档案馆全宗指出，新经济政策时期俄国东正教会革新派（“活教会”）的全面溃败，不能仅归咎于国家的冷酷利用，更源于普通正教平信徒在基层将其定性为破坏神圣秩序的“布尔什维克基督教异端”。革新派推行公历改历、俄语礼拜、神父再婚与反修院改革，严重冲击了农民与平信徒借传统圣礼仪轨体验“神圣临在”的文化认知，制造出剧烈认知失调。即便1923年6月当局释放牧首吉洪转向“分而治之”、革新派亦退回传统主教制，平信徒仍借切断供奉与拒绝圣礼彻底孤立革新派；而守旧教会虽以政治效忠换取教义纯洁，却因自我隔绝于苏维埃公共生活，导致其在1930年代国家全面打击面前丧失社会盟友与防线。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **国家战术利用与1923年6月转向（pp. 616–617, 626–627）**：`1921-05-09 [N.S.]`卢那察尔斯基电呈列宁（RTsKhIDNI f. 5, op. 1, d. 120）倡议秘密扶植“苏维埃东正教”打击守旧牧首；`1922-05`革新派（VTsU）借国家暗助迫吉洪让位并仿党组织在教区设“支部”，于`1923-04..05`公会达顶峰。`1923-06`苏维埃政权因国际舆论及忌惮“苏维埃东正教会”真能自立，释放吉洪并转行“分而治之”：`1924-01-02`反宗教委员会与司法部下令按教派人数分割/物理隔断教堂并按比例课重税，同时由GPU强迫“合并”却暗阻和解，坐实革新派作为国家毁教工具的污名 `[ch03–ch04, ch13–ch14]`。
2. **“异端”社会学：圣礼临在观与基层经济抵制（pp. 618–625, 628–634）**：借助George Zito“认知失调”与Guy Swanson“神圣临在（immanence）”理论，作者证明东正教信众的救赎与农村节日周期紧密绑定传统礼仪与儒略历。平信徒视革新派为“穿法衣的政委”与敌基督，尽管革新派1923年改组为“神圣主教会议”并恢复旧历礼仪，各地仍以扣留薪俸、停供木柴与抵制收费圣礼实施经济围困——`1925-10`北德维纳大教堂季收入仅`32`卢布，神父月入跌至`10`卢布，`1926`年坦波夫部分地区革新派教堂归零；同时国家仍将革新派神职划为“被剥夺公民权者（*lishentsy*）”，使其陷入两端受敌的“无人区” `[ch05–ch11, ch15–ch21]`。
3. **1927年谢尔吉妥协与NEP文化封闭结局（pp. 625–626, 634–635）**：`1927-07-29 [N.S.]`谢尔吉效忠声明以政治顺从换取守旧教会内部自治，巩固了“在教会之外容忍社会主义、唯独在教会内绝不容忍”的堡垒心态；伴随至1928年约`4,650万`人（1.3亿人口中）非宗教化及浸信会吸收宗教革新热情，守旧东正教最终孤立无援 `[ch12, ch14, ch21]`。

## 原文核验引文（Verbatim Quotes）
> "Although popular religious belief led to concrete local action that political leaders misinterpreted as "counterrevolutionary," Orthodox laity neither desired nor formed organized opposition to the new Soviet state. They were counterrevolutionary in the cultural sphere, however, insofar as their defense of the old religious system resisted the changes inherent in the Sovietization of Russian society." `[p. 614 / PDF p. 2 | sources/articles/roslof_1996_heresy_of_bolshevik_christianity_nep/ch01_p_615.md#L20-L25]`

> "Renovationism's incongruence with Orthodox tradition initially provoked resistance from rank-and-file believers that the government interpreted as anti-Soviet and, therefore, counterrevolutionary. That "political" resistance was directly related to the content of the reforms themselves." `[p. 620 / PDF p. 8 | sources/articles/roslof_1996_heresy_of_bolshevik_christianity_nep/ch07_p_621.md#L41-L46]`

> "Rather, Orthodox believers are saved by the proper and corporate completion of liturgical acts in which God himself is present. Renovationist clergy performed the salvific rites but behaved in ways that showed they did not believe in the immanence of those rites. They changed the words, the actions, and even the time-the calendar-that undergird the laity's understanding of sacred immanence." `[p. 624 / PDF p. 12 | sources/articles/roslof_1996_heresy_of_bolshevik_christianity_nep/ch11_p_625.md#L11-L17]`

> "A major difference between the two main Orthodox factions was that the renovationists hoped to help build socialism in all of Soviet Russia, including the church, whereas the Tikhonites tolerated socialism everywhere except in the church. ... Because nonrenovationist Orthodox divorced religious belief from active involvement in Soviet society and culture of the 1920s, they lacked both allies and defenses in the battles that decimated the institutional church in the 1930s and eliminated religion from the public sphere until the very end of the Soviet era." `[p. 635 / PDF p. 23 | sources/articles/roslof_1996_heresy_of_bolshevik_christianity_nep/ch21_p_635.md#L80-L84, #L97-L102]`
