## 核心论断命题（Thesis）

Siegelbaum 与 Suny 论证：1917–1929 年间俄国工人经历“作为自主反抗资本阶级的集体”之解体，又在苏维埃党国规训下被**再造**为名义上的统治阶级与忠诚的主体——内战造成人口锐减、“去阶级化”与工厂委员会服从于党委员会；NEP 恢复人数却削弱了阶级作为 empowerment 能指；1928 年后大工业化与“流沙社会”式流动使阶级认同更多表现为**对政权的政治忠诚构造**，而非车间自组织传统意义上的 class-for-itself。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）

1. **解体（unmaking）**：1917 工业工人约 300 万，1920 年底略逾 100 万；幸存工人“精疲力尽”，不再作为“强健好斗的社会阶级”登台；布尔什维克以党组织取代苏维埃/工厂委员会主导的“无产阶级民主”（Deutscher、Fitzpatrick、Keep 引述，`pp. 14–15, 625–655`）。
2. **悖论性存续**：Chase 的“分裂阶级”、Koenker 的“意识向邻里/世俗文化迁移”质疑简单“去无产阶级化”叙事（`pp. 15–17`）。
3. **NEP 再造话语而非车间团结**：1920 年代工业劳动力恢复，但“工人阶级”符号失去锋芒；阶级越来越像忠于政权目标的**政治建构**，而非共同经济行动的基底（`pp. 18–20, 893–915`）。
4. **大转变（remaking under state）**：1928–1932 集体化与工业化制造 Lewin 所谓“quicksand society”——农民变工人、工人升干部再落马、城市“农村化”；官方再扩“无产阶级”定义（含社会出身），以视觉与动员艺术重塑革命工人神话（`pp. 17, 776–781`, `pp. 926–942`）。

> —— Isaac Deutscher, cited in `sources/monographs/siegelbaum_suny_1994_making_workers_soviet/ch03_class_backwards_in_search_of_the_soviet.md` [Book p. 14 / PDF p. 30, lines 627–628]

> "Workers who had survived the associated traumas were 'exhausted and prostrate'; they no longer cohered as a 'vigorous and militant social class,' and had 'disappeared from the political stage.' They were, in a word, 'declassed.'"
> —— `sources/monographs/siegelbaum_suny_1994_making_workers_soviet/ch03_class_backwards_in_search_of_the_soviet.md` [Book pp. 14–15 / PDF pp. 30–31, lines 651–655]

> "Our search for a Soviet working class has come up against a paradox. In the course of the 1920s, the industrial workforce was restored: yet the working class as a signifier of empowerment lost its salience. … Rather than assume that class was something that was fragmenting or pulling apart, we might do better to conceive of it as an emerging political construct whose employment signified loyalty to the regime and its goals."
> —— `sources/monographs/siegelbaum_suny_1994_making_workers_soviet/ch03_class_backwards_in_search_of_the_soviet.md` [Book pp. 18–19 / PDF pp. 35–36, lines 893–902]

> "Throughout the years of the First Five-Year Plan, the USSR was, in Moshe Lewin's words, a 'quicksand society,' characterized by flux, uncertainty, mobility, and anomie. Peasants became workers, workers became bosses, bosses frequently found themselves in prison. As the countryside disgorged its millions, the cities were 'ruralized.'"
> —— `sources/monographs/siegelbaum_suny_1994_making_workers_soviet/ch03_class_backwards_in_search_of_the_soviet.md` [Book p. 20 / PDF p. 36, lines 932–936]

## 语境说明与局限提示

- 本条概括导论对西方史学争鸣的综述，非单一档案研究的实证结论；各章（Chase、Koenker、Ward、Kotkin 等）对 1920–1930 年代工人能动性有不同评估。
- “去阶级化”叙事与 Koenker **deproletarianization** 批判并置时，应区分**人口数量**与**政治组织形态**两维。
- 1930 年代是否仍宜称“工人阶级”，Rittersporn 章主张 **urban laboring mass** 标签可能更贴切，延伸见阅读札记 RN-W13。
