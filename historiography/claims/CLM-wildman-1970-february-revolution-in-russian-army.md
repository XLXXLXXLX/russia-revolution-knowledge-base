## 1. 核心论断（中文精炼）
艾伦·怀尔德曼（Allan Wildman）借助军官日记、战壕检查报告、大本营电报与 1917 年 3 月报刊，重构了二月革命冲击前线七百万俄军的动态轨迹。他指出，1916–1917 年冬战壕士兵虽怨恨军官与宫廷谣言，却仍受军纪约束而未酝酿兵变；首都哗变及《第一号命令》随铁路与电报席卷各战线后，虽引发撤换军官、拒行敬礼与返乡分地逃兵潮，但 1917 年 3 月前线并无私刑杀官，反而在杜马特派员调停与官兵妥协建委下出现暂时稳定，临时政府威望一度高于苏维埃。前线士兵至 4 月中旬决定性倒向苏维埃，并非布尔什维克鼓动所致，而是“资产阶级”报刊指责工兵、坚持“打到最后胜利”口号及米留可夫 4 月 18 日照会激化士兵对新政府阶级性质与和平诚意的深刻疑虑所致。

## 2. 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
> "However onerous conditions were at the front in the winter of 1916/17, it could not be said that the trench soldiers were on the verge of revolt. ... More ominous than complaints over the hardships of war was the rapidly declining prestige of the imperial family and the governmental leadership." `[p. 5 / PDF p. 4]` (`sources/articles/wildman_1970_february_revolution_russian_army/ch03_p_5.md#L33-L51`)

> "The phenomena described above can be characterized as the spontaneous reaction of untutored trench soldiers to the new political situation; they often occurred under the stimulus of news from the capital, and cannot justly be ascribed to the pernicious influence of the Petrograd Soviet or to any other organized group of agitators. However, there was one Soviet measure which profoundly affected the further course of the upheaval at the front and thoroughly deserves its awesome reputation. This was Order No. 1." `[p. 13 / PDF p. 12]` (`sources/articles/wildman_1970_february_revolution_russian_army/ch11_p_13.md#L21-L33`)

> "In all the numerous memoirs, diaries and other accounts, the author has not come across a single instance of a lynching at the front lines in the first few weeks of March." `[p. 16 fn. 29 / PDF p. 15]` (`sources/articles/wildman_1970_february_revolution_russian_army/ch14_p_16.md#L64-L66`)

> "In the first few weeks the Provisional Government and its representatives still enjoyed tremendous prestige at the front and commanded the attention of the soldiers far more than did the Soviet. By the second week in April this situation was reversed and the Soviet emerged as the sole authoritative organ for the 'revolutionary democracy' at the front as well as at the rear, while a pall of suspicion hung over the 'bourgeois' Provisional Government." `[p. 22 / PDF p. 21]` (`sources/articles/wildman_1970_february_revolution_russian_army/ch20_p_22.md#L33-L40`)

> "A blunder like Milyukov's note to the Allies of 18 April (assuring them of Russia's faithfulness to the secret treaties) was guaranteed to effect a shift in loyalty, but it was only the final episode of this drama. This process had very little to do with Bolshevik propaganda (arriving on 4 April, Lenin needed some weeks to re-orient his own followers), but a good deal to do with agitation in the 'bourgeois' press over the workers' slackness in war production, the irresponsible power of the Soviet, and the breakdown of discipline in the army during the last week in March. It was this clamour transmitted to the front by monarchist and Kadet-oriented officers which backfired, arousing, instead of the hoped-for well of patriotism, deep-seated anxieties about the class character of the Provisional Government." `[p. 23 / PDF p. 22]` (`sources/articles/wildman_1970_february_revolution_russian_army/ch21_p_23.md#L14-L25`)

## 3. 经验依据与档案依托
1. **大本营与各战线消息传导迟滞**：`1917-02-28 [O.S.] / 03-13 [N.S.]` 正午 Alekseev 向方面军司令通报首都哗变但未命传达基层；西南、罗马尼亚与高加索方面军迟至 `03-03 [O.S.]` 才获正式通知；`03-03` 凌晨签字退位因 Rodzianko 要求等候米哈伊尔宣言而延迟至 `03-04` 06:00 放行，多数部队至 **`03-05 [O.S.] / 03-18 [N.S.]`** 方才获悉，引发士兵怀疑军官隐瞒“和平分地法令”（`pp. 7–9`；引 *Krasnyi arkhiv* 电报与 Selivachev/Boldyrev 日记）。
2. **《第一号命令》电传与前线秩序量化重估**：《第一号命令》由总参谋部电台发往各前线（“致所有人”，见 Miller 与 Kolchak 证词及 Lukomsky 回忆录），被军官误作新政府命令；`03-05 [O.S.]` 的《第二号命令》鲜为前线知晓（`pp. 14–15`）。但私刑集中于后方基地与波罗的海舰队（喀琅施塔得 Lukomsky 报 `60` 人 / Pepelaev 报约 `100` 人，赫尔辛基 `39` 人，彼得格勒约 `20` 人），**1917 年 3 月前数周前线战壕私刑为零**；Lukomsky 估 3 月逃兵每日每师 `5–7` 人（月 `1%–2%`，全军 `750 万` 人中约 `10–15 万`），且多属听信分地谣言的爱国暂离者，后在 `04-15 [O.S.] / 04-28 [N.S.]` 大赦前部分自愿归队（`pp. 16–17`）。
3. **三月中旬稳定与四月逆转**：Alekseev `03-09 [O.S.]` 致 Lvov 电报刻意捏造军心仍忠君（`p. 8` fn. 11），而杜马代表（如北方战线 Yanushkevich 与 Filonenko、赫尔辛基 Skobelev 与 Rodichev）与将领（Radko-Dmitriev、Everts、Brusilov）合作促成 3 月底前线各级委员会建制化，一度稳住战线；最终破坏这一脆弱平衡的是 3 月末“资产阶级”报刊与主战军官话语及 `04-18 [O.S.] / 05-01 [N.S.]` 米留可夫照会（`pp. 18–23`）。

## 4. 方法论价值与可能盲区
- **方法论价值**：将“双重政权”与革命转折的分析视野从彼得格勒陶里达宫拓展到 2,000 英里前线，既有力修正了白军回忆录“二月及第一号命令一夜毁灭全军”的事后宿命论，也为费吉斯（1996）日后深化“战壕布尔什维主义”与社会等级倒转提供了坚实分期底座。
- **可能盲区**：作者声明写于 1968 年美国馆藏资料基础上，未及直接查阅苏联中央军事历史档案馆连队级原始档案（后在其 1980/1987 年两卷本专著中补足）；此外，论文尚未对四月至六月攻势间“和平 vs 战争”舆论转向展开具体微观展开（留待后续专论）。
