## 1. 核心论断（中文精炼）
史蒂文·古西尔（Steven L. Guthier）系统汇算 1917 年 11–12 月乌克兰八省、十大城市、顿巴斯矿区及城市工人苏维埃执委会选举数据，结合 1897 与 1926 年人口普查，修正了“乌克兰农民缺乏民族意识”的旧见，揭示了 1917 年乌克兰民族主义的“单阶级社会基础”及其结构困局。在乡村，88% 的农民为乌克兰族，而地主、官僚与商工阶层多属非乌民族；乌克兰社会革命党依托“全乌农民联盟”将捍卫个体继承农地、反对俄式村社均分及推行乌语学校结合，在十大城市外夺得 55% 选票（含左翼联合名单共 71%）。相反，城市与顿巴斯工业无产阶级高度俄罗斯化（1917 年 9 月基辅平民中乌族仅占 16.4%），卫戍农兵亦迅速逃亡返乡分地，使布尔什维克等全俄政党牢控城市苏维埃与工业交通命脉；乌克兰革命并非败于缺乏民众基础，而是败于纯农民选民难以克服的组织与城市资源匮乏。

## 2. 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
> "The Constituent Assembly election demonstrated that Ukrainian nationalism had a strong popular base in the Ukrainian village. In the eight provinces discussed above, 55 percent of all votes cast outside the Ukraine's ten largest cities went to lists dominated by the UPSR and Selians'ka Spilka; another 16 percent went to Left PSR/UPSR slates." `[p. 40 / PDF p. 12]` (`sources/articles/guthier_1979_ukrainian_nationalism_1917/ch11_p_40.md#L15-L21`)

> "However, this discontent did not translate into rejection of the Ukrainian national cause. The government parties in the Rada, the USDRP, and liberal Socialist Federalists, fared badly among the peasantry in the election. The UPSR, which itself was increasingly critical of the social policies of the Rada, retained its influence in the village and overwhelmed the Bolsheviks in the election." `[p. 40 / PDF p. 12]` (`sources/articles/guthier_1979_ukrainian_nationalism_1917/ch11_p_40.md#L31-L39`)

> "However, the Ukrainian peasant troops in the garrisons constituted a transitory force in the politics of the cities. War weariness stimulated a high desertion rate. The troops were also anxious to return to the villages in order to defend their interests during the spontaneous land reform being carried out by the peasantry. Consequently, the Ukrainian soldiers melted back quickly into the village, leaving the national cause in the cities in a more disadvantaged position than suggested by table 3." `[p. 43 / PDF p. 15]` (`sources/articles/guthier_1979_ukrainian_nationalism_1917/ch14_p_43.md#L60-L69`)

> "The elections also demonstrated that Ukrainian nationalism as a substantial political force was a one-class movement. Ukrainians were poorly represented among the urban bourgeoisie and proletariat; moreover, these classes were the most Russianized segments of Ukrainian society. ... In the radical climate of 1917, the weakness of the Ukrainian movement among the proletariat proved even more crucial. It provided the Bolsheviks with their entering wedge into the Ukraine. The Donbass and the great industrial cities became the base of support for the Bolsheviks in their struggle with the Ukrainian national Socialists." `[p. 46 / PDF p. 18]` (`sources/articles/guthier_1979_ukrainian_nationalism_1917/ch17_p_46.md#L54-L58, L66-L72`)

> "The Ukrainian revolution failed not through the lack of a popular base, but through the organizational problems and resource deficiencies arising from its overwhelmingly peasant constituency." `[p. 47 / PDF p. 19]` (`sources/articles/guthier_1979_ukrainian_nationalism_1917/ch18_p_47.md#L32-L38`)

## 3. 经验依据与档案依托
1. **普查阶级—民族结构**：1897 年普查中 `87%` 乌克兰人务农，仅 `2.4%` 居于 2 万人以上城市，`93%` 为农民等级；农村识字率 `1/7`（1926 年仅 `2/5`）。1905 年右岸三省 `96.5%`、波尔塔瓦 `82.1%` 农户实行世袭户地制，故 UPSR 与 *Selians'ka Spilka*（机关报 *Narodnia volia* 日印从 `1 万` 增至 `20 万` 份）能有效利用反村社“平均主义”与乌语学校诉求（`pp. 31–34`）。
2. **1917 年 11–12 月立宪会议选举分省与城乡拆分**（`pp. 36–43`，据 Radkey 1950、Spirin 1968 及地方报刊）：八省核心区波尔塔瓦 `80.5%`（市外 `83.2%`）、基辅 `77%`（市外 `>83%`）、波多利亚 `79%`、沃里尼亚 `71%`（布党与 PSR 均 `<5%`），切尔尼戈夫 `49.7%`、叶卡捷琳诺斯拉夫 `46.6%`（布党 `18%` 中 `80%` 来自顿巴斯与工业城），哈尔科夫与赫尔松左翼 PSR/UPSR 分别获 `72.8%` 与 `53%`。而在十大城市，扣除占乌党选票 `29%–53%` 的驻军票后（Table 4–5），乌党在平民中均沦为少数（基辅 `20%` 不敌俄人选民 `29%`，敖德萨 `18%` 不敌锡安派 `34%` 与布党 `19%`，哈尔科夫仅 `8%`，叶卡捷琳诺斯拉夫 `13%` 不敌布党 `27%`）。
3. **顿巴斯与城市苏维埃力量对比**（`pp. 44–45`, Table 6–7）：乌族占顿巴斯矿工冶金工约 `1/3`，但乌社得票不足 `1/10`（顿巴斯矿区布党 `17,075` vs 乌社 `889`）；7 大城市工人/工兵苏维埃执委会中布尔什维克与其他俄国社会主义者占绝对统治（基辅 `30` 席中布 `18`、俄社 `10`、乌派仅 `2`；叶纳基耶沃 `40` 席中乌派 `0`、布 `31`）。

## 4. 方法论价值与可能盲区
- **方法论价值**：通过剥离驻军选票与城乡选区，精准实证 1917 年乌克兰革命的政治地理裂痕，不仅有力修正了“拉达缺乏群众基础”及“农民批评拉达即拥护布党”的苏联史学叙述，也为 George Liber（1992）解释 1920 年代苏维埃“城市乌克兰化”的必要性奠定前史基准。
- **可能盲区**：(1) 本文未纳入克里米亚之外的塔夫里达省大陆乌族县数据（因材料不足，见 `p. 31` fn. 3）；(2) 在哈尔科夫与赫尔松，UPSR 与左翼 PSR 合提联合名单，难以从纯选票上精确剥离选民对“民族自治”与“激进土地/和平”的权重占比；(3) 库藏 PDF 第 8 页（`p. [36]` Table 1）右侧边缘轻微裁切，导致 `Kadet` 列数字末位缺失，引用时须保留缺位标记不得补造。
