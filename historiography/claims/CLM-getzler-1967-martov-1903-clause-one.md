## 核心论断命题（Thesis）
格茨勒认为，1903 年俄国社会民主工党第二次代表大会上围绕党章第一条（党员资格条款）的争论，绝非纯粹权力席位争夺的马克思主义经院式“烟幕弹”，而是一场严肃的、酝酿已久的**两种政党观念的公开对决**：马尔托夫在此首次公开挑战列宁并系统确立了孟什维主义的组织基本原则——将秘密的职业革命家骨干组织嵌入由先进工人和同情者组成的广大群众性社会民主党之中（以崩得为参照），拒绝列宁《怎么办？》式由绝对服从中央的职业革命家精英“拳头”构成的全封闭政党模型。

## 论证链条与依据（Reasoning & Evidence）
1. **会前思想先兆的历时性考证**：格茨勒指出，马尔托夫在 1903 年 4 月发表于罗莎·卢森堡主编的《社会民主主义评论》（*Przegląd Socjaldemokratyczny*）的文章及同年 4–5 月起草的 39 条党章草案中，已明确提出要在沙俄秘密斗争需求与吸纳广大工人群众之间寻求折中，力图消除“集中制的过度膨胀”（a hypertrophy of centralism），因此第一条条文并非大会现场临时拼凑的派系工具（`ch03`, `[pp. 68–71, 78]`）。
2. **权力冲突作为催化剂而非唯一动因**：格茨勒并不否认《火星报》组织闭门预备会围绕中央委员会席位（“硬派”与“软派”对立、克拉斯科夫与亚历山德罗娃之争、组织委员会被摧毁）的尖锐权力搏斗加剧了第一条辩论的火药味与不妥协性，但他强调不能以此消解双方在党章观念上的实质分歧（`ch03`, `[pp. 77–78]`）。
3. **两条组织路线的结构对比**：援引《俄社民工党二大速记》（*Vtoroi s'ezd: Protokoly*, pp. 263, 271）及托洛茨基《西伯利亚代表团报告》，指出马尔托夫希望将“党员”称号扩展至在党组织指导下工作的罢工者与示威者（秘密组织充当驱动广大工人政党的“飞轮”），正如崩得在犹太无产阶级中所实践的那样；而列宁则据传字面意义上挥舞“拳头”（fist）作为中央委员会的政治象征，将党限定为《火星报》代办员网络式的严格纪律精英团（`ch04_pages_101-125.md#L1-L30`, `[pp. 78–79]`）。

## 原文直引（Verbatim Quote）
> "Yet it would be a mistake to regard the membership paragraph as a mere red herring and the debate as nothing more than a struggle for power taking the peculiar form of a tournament of Marxist dialecticians fighting with the weapons of rival formulas, sharpened razor thin. If we grant that Martov in the months before the convocation of the congress was feeling his way to a wider concept of the party, while Lenin stood fast for the principles of *Iskra* militant and of his élitist *What is to be Done?* and wanted them embodied and realized in the new party which was to issue from the Second Congress, then the wrangle over the membership paragraph must be taken seriously as a genuine debate over rival conceptions of the party." `[p. 78]`

> "This debate was the occasion when Martov, then twenty-nine years old, came of age, first challenged Lenin in public, and formulated the basic organizational principle of what became Menshevism: the idea that Russian social democrats should refuse to remain an organization of professional revolutionaries pure and simple, because Russian conditions had hitherto imposed this on them, and that they should strive to attract the advanced sections of the working masses into a social democratic party." `[p. 78]`

> "The workers' party envisaged by Martov consisted of the organized professional revolutionaries 'plus the sum total of the active advanced elements of the proletariat'. What he and Axelrod and their supporters really had in mind was something like the Bund which had succeeded in embedding its excellent organization of professional revolutionaries in a large, active, and widespread party of the masses of the Jewish proletariat." `[p. 79]`
