# 伊拉克利·策烈铁里（Irakli Georgievich Tsereteli）

## [T1] 60秒定性微摘要
格鲁吉亚孟什维克领袖，1917年彼得格勒苏维埃“革命护国主义”总设计师。3月自西伯利亚流放归来后主导苏维埃执委会“星室”，力推联立政府与六月攻势以维系同自由派的同盟；因经济瘫痪与镇压布尔什维克而流失工人支持，十月革命后返格鲁吉亚并在流亡中著述。

## [T2] 核心脉络、时效关系边表与史学争鸣光谱

### 2.1 核心历史脉络
- **第二届国家杜马声望与西伯利亚流放（1881–1917.03）**：生于格鲁吉亚库塔伊西（`1881-11-20 [O.S.] / 1881-12-02 [N.S.]`）。1907年任第二届国家杜马社会民主党党团主席，因雄辩演说享誉全俄；“六三政变”后被沙皇法庭判处苦役，刑满后转西伯利亚伊尔库茨克省定居流放（前后失去自由近十年，至1917年二月革命获释），期间形成“西伯利亚齐美尔瓦尔德派”立场，把苏维埃设想为凝聚全民各阶层的广泛民主论坛而非狭隘的阶级专政机关。
- **返抵彼得格勒与“革命护国主义”定调（1917.03–04）**：`1917-03-21 [O.S.] / 1917-04-03 [N.S.]` 返抵彼得格勒，迅速取代左倾动摇的苏汉诺夫、斯捷克洛夫，与费奥多尔·但（F. I. Dan）、齐赫泽（N. S. Chkheidze）、沃伊京斯基（V. S. Voitinskii）、戈茨（A. R. Gots）组成把持彼得格勒苏维埃执行委员会的核心“星室”（Star Chamber）。他提出“革命护国主义”（Революционное оборончество），主张在通过国际民主压力争取普遍和平的同时必须维持前线军队战斗力，并强调俄国资产阶级民主革命的存亡系于同“国家有生力量”（живые силы страны，含进步资产阶级与自由派知识分子）的结盟，坚决反对苏维埃单独夺权以防触发小资产阶级与农民反叛。
- **入阁第一届联立政府与政权泥潭（1917.05–06）**：四月危机后，为挽救濒临崩溃的临时政府，策烈铁里打破孟什维克禁止入阁的传统教条，于 `1917-05-05 [O.S.] / 1917-05-18 [N.S.]` 出任第一届联立政府邮电部长（7月兼代内务部长），但坚持社会主义者在15名阁员中只占6席少数，以免吓跑自由派资产阶级。入阁后，为维护同立宪民主党的同盟，他被迫背书克伦斯基的“六月攻势”，并在 `1917-06-11 [O.S.] / 1917-06-24 [N.S.]` 联席会议上强硬要求解除布尔什维克与亲布尔什维克部队的武装，与费奥多尔·但及马尔托夫发生激烈冲突。
- **七月事变、权威破产与晚年流亡（1917.07–1959）**：`1917-07-03 [O.S.] / 1917-07-16 [N.S.]` 至 `07-05 [O.S.] / 07-18 [N.S.]` 七月事变期间，策烈铁里拒绝塔夫利达宫外数十万工人与士兵“全部政权归苏维埃”的呼声，亦否决马尔托夫建立“清一色社会主义政府”的方案，并在7月5日拂晓与但高唱《马赛曲》欢迎入城镇压的忠于临时政府部队。`1917-07-23 [O.S.] / 1917-08-05 [N.S.]` 第二届联立政府组建时，他退出内阁转回苏维埃全俄中央执委会（VTsIK），但随科尔尼洛夫叛乱爆发，其“有生力量同盟”战略彻底破产。十月革命后，他曾在 `1918-01-05 [O.S.] / 1918-01-18 [N.S.]` 全俄立宪会议上发表长篇反布尔什维克演说；随后返回孟什维克执政的格鲁吉亚民主共和国（1918–1921），红军入格后流亡巴黎与纽约，撰写巨著《二月革命回忆录》（*Воспоминания о Февральской революции*），1959年5月20日卒于纽约。

### 2.3 史学争鸣光谱（Historiographical Spectrum）
- **社会史修正派与组织疏离论（Ziva Galili, 1989）**：加利利在《孟什维克领袖与俄国工人》中指出，策烈铁里的悲剧并非源于简单的“软弱”或“背叛”，而是源于其西伯利亚流放时期形成的政治认知与1917年彼得格勒社会极化现实的错位。他深信没有资产阶级参与的社会主义政权必然重蹈1905年孤立溃败的覆辙，因此把维系同自由派的联立置于首位；但当工业家采取关厂停工、临时政府推迟改革并发动六月攻势时，策烈铁里作为入阁部长沦为工人眼中的“缓兵之计调停人”（buffer），亲手切断了孟什维克与工厂基层工人的阶级纽带（参见 `CLM-galili-1989-revolutionary-defensism`、`CLM-galili-1989-coalition-government-trap`、`CLM-galili-1989-menshevik-worker-alienation`）。
- **西方政治传记学派（W. H. Roobol, 1976）**：鲁博尔在《策烈铁里：俄国革命中的民主主义者》（*Tsereteli — A Democrat in the Russian Revolution*）中将其定位为一位原则坚定的议会民主主义者，认为他在1917年寻求的跨阶级民主共识是避免俄国滑向内战与一党专政的唯一制度性尝试，但在缺乏中间派社会基础与战争崩溃的极端环境下注定难以为继。
- **孟什维克左翼与当事人反思（J. Martov / N. Sukhanov vs. I. Tsereteli, 1963）**：马尔托夫与苏汉诺夫批评策烈铁里被“国家责任感”绑架，为了迁就已失去社会根基的立宪民主党而背弃了苏维埃民主群众；策烈铁里本人在《二月革命回忆录》中则辩护称，在1917年俄国生产力与农民占多数的条件下，脱离自由派与技术精英的“清一色社会主义政府”只会立即演变为布尔什维克式的恐怖专政。

## [T3] 深度考证、分期细目与原文引证档案

### 3.1 西伯利亚流放遗产与“革命护国主义”的理论构建（1917年3月–4月）
1. **“星室”（Star Chamber）接管苏维埃领导权**：
   - 1917年3月中旬前，彼得格勒苏维埃执行委员会由苏汉诺夫、斯捷克洛夫等左翼知识分子主导，对临时政府采取严苛监督但拒绝担责的模糊姿态。3月21日（公历4月3日）策烈铁里率西伯利亚流放者返抵彼得格勒后，立即将“革命护国主义”确立为苏维埃多数派的指导纲领，并在4月初推动执委会改组，建立由策烈铁里、但、齐赫泽、戈茨、沃伊京斯基等人组成的常务局（被反对派讥为“星室”），全面掌控苏维埃外交与内政议程 `[Galili 1989, pp. 131-136, 158-159 / PDF pp. 149-154, 184-185]`。
2. **核心原典引证：苏维埃作为全民族团结的讲坛而非阶级专政工具**：
   - 对接 `CLM-galili-1989-revolutionary-defensism`（[CLM-galili-1989-revolutionary-defensism.md](file:///C:/Users/lsaax/.t3/projects/russian-revolution-knowledge-base/historiography/claims/CLM-galili-1989-revolutionary-defensism.md)）：
   > "More importantly still, in the isolated reality of Irkutsk, where industrial and social conflict barely showed their faces, Tsereteli came to expect the soviets to arise as 'organs of struggle against the external obstacle, against the autocratic regime, and of efforts to draw all strata of the population into this struggle.' The soviets' chief dynamic, as the Siberian Zimmerwaldists projected it, lay not in expressing the separate needs and moods of the workers, not in accentuating class division within urban society, but simply in providing a broad forum— broader and more democratic than the duma committees or the War Industries Committees—for the effort of the entire nation to shake off the autocratic regime and rescue the country from defeat." `[Galili 1989, p. 136 / PDF p. 154]`
   > "In Tsereteli's understanding, the revolution could follow its continuous operational path precisely because the dual commitments of all the 'vital forces' of the country—to defend Russia against defeat and to construct in it a democratic system of government—provided a sound and durable basis for class allies." `[Galili 1989, pp. 149-150 / PDF pp. 175-176]`
   > "He insisted that as long as the bourgeoisie did not discard the democratic banner under which the revolution was made, the proletariat was obliged to recognize the governmental executive (which had been empowered through an agreement with the soviet) as 'embodying the sovereign will of the revolutionary nation.'" `[Galili 1989, p. 151 / PDF p. 177]`

### 3.2 第一届联立政府的入阁逻辑与“调停人陷阱”（1917年5月–7月）
1. **刻意维持社会主义阁员少数地位的制度算计**：
   - 四月危机（米留可夫照会危机）后，策烈铁里最初与但一致反对入阁，但面对临时政府威信扫地、前线瓦解的危局，策烈铁里在 `1917-05-01 [O.S.] / 1917-05-14 [N.S.]` 说服苏维埃执委会批准入阁。他在谈判中坚决要求社会主义者在15名内阁成员中只占6席（克伦斯基、策烈铁里、斯科别列夫、切尔诺夫、佩舍霍诺夫、佩列维尔泽夫），理由是一旦社会主义者占据多数，资产阶级将拒绝承担责任，而苏维埃又缺乏独立管理国家经济与军队的行政机器 `[Galili 1989, pp. 169-181 / PDF pp. 195-207]`。
   - 对接 `CLM-galili-1989-coalition-government-trap`（[CLM-galili-1989-coalition-government-trap.md](file:///C:/Users/lsaax/.t3/projects/russian-revolution-knowledge-base/historiography/claims/CLM-galili-1989-coalition-government-trap.md)）：
   > "Dan and Tsereteli objected strongly to the idea that the soviet take power into its hands, whether directly or by seizing a majority in the coalition government. Even as Tsereteli called inside the Executive Committee for reversing the decision on coalition, he argued vehemently that the socialist parties should claim only a minority of the ministerial portfolios so as neither to frighten the bourgeoisie away from collaboration with the Democracy nor to encourage extravagant expectations on the part of the masses." `[Galili 1989, p. 169 / PDF p. 195]`
   > "Tsereteli argued against reading the coalition move as the scheduling of a socialist stage for the revolution. Indeed, their determination to guard the coalition from disruption and the bourgeoisie from alienation became for the Defensist leaders the central preoccupation, overshadowing any immediate concern for the concrete aims listed in the platform that they themselves had written." `[Galili 1989, p. 181 / PDF p. 207]`
2. **经济危机、6月11日“解除武装”风波与七月事变决裂**：
   - 进入1917年6月，彼得格勒资方拒绝让步并威胁停工，孟什维克劳工部长斯科别列夫与邮电部长策烈铁里一再呼吁工人克制工资诉求以防“吓跑工业家”，导致布尔什维克在工厂委员会与区苏维埃迅速夺取多数 `[Galili 1989, pp. 295-303 / PDF pp. 321-329]`。
   - 在6月10日布尔什维克原定武装游行流产后，策烈铁里在6月11日执委会与全俄苏维埃一大主席团联席会议上激烈主张宣布布尔什维克为“反革命阴谋者”并收缴其武器（遭但与马尔托夫阻止） `[Galili 1989, pp. 308-311 / PDF pp. 334-337]`。七月事变（`1917-07-03 [O.S.] / 1917-07-16 [N.S.]` 至 `07-05 [O.S.] / 07-18 [N.S.]`）平息并经历7月5日清晨士官生捣毁《真理报》及7月7日李沃夫辞职后，策烈铁里短暂兼任代理内务部长，主持对布尔什维克与激进左翼的行政弹压（期间政府羁押托洛茨基、卢那察尔斯基与布尔什维克中央委员加米涅夫等），彻底完成从革命调停者向秩序卫道士的角色转换 `[Galili 1989, pp. 333-336 / PDF pp. 359-362]`。
   - 对接 `CLM-galili-1989-menshevik-worker-alienation`（[CLM-galili-1989-menshevik-worker-alienation.md](file:///C:/Users/lsaax/.t3/projects/russian-revolution-knowledge-base/historiography/claims/CLM-galili-1989-menshevik-worker-alienation.md)）：
   > "In effect, the soviet organs of mediation, headed by the Defensist Minister of Labor, M. I. Skobelev, found themselves forced to perform as 'buffers,' absorbing the conflict while preventing a real collision until the state's regulatory system could be put in place." `[Galili 1989, p. 299 / PDF p. 325]`
   > "Most rank-and-file workers clearly did not perceive the broader rationale behind the constant exhortations against strikes, seeing instead a change in the Defensist leadership's attitudes since the creation of the coalition and blaming that shift on the leaders' reluctance to offend their bourgeois partners." `[Galili 1989, p. 301 / PDF p. 327]`
   > "Indeed, the workers' desire during the spring and summer of 1917 for a purely socialist government without the Bolsheviks in dominant roles—a view revealed in many factory resolutions from May through August—can be traced closely to their sense that immediate takeover of economic reconstruction by a socialist government was crucial if they were to keep the upper hand and not be brought down by starvation and unemployment. Had the Mensheviks led in creating such a government as early as June or July, that move not only would have preserved and increased the considerable pocket of political support they had already won among the workers; it might also have arrested the trend that drove other workers to extreme moods and impatient action..." `[Galili 1989, p. 398 / PDF p. 424]`
