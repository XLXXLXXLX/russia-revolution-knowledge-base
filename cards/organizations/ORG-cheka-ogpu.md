## [T1] 60秒定性微摘要
苏俄与苏联早期政治警察机关（1917–1934），由捷尔任斯基创立。历经全俄肃反委员会（ВЧК，1917–1922）、国家政治保卫局（ГПУ，1922–1923）与国家政治保卫总局（ОГПУ，1923–1934）三阶段，从法外非常机构演变为直属政治局的常设宪制部门，执行红色恐怖与强制集体化。

## [T2] 核心脉络、时效关系边表与史学争鸣光谱

### 2.1 核心历史脉络
- **法外行政起源与直属人民委员会的宪制设计（1917.12–1918.06）**：`1917-12-05 [O.S.] / 1917-12-18 [N.S.]` 彼得格勒军事革命委员会（MRC）解散后，面对全俄公务员罢工危机，捷尔任斯基于 `1917-12-07 [O.S.] / 1917-12-20 [N.S.]` 向人民委员会（Sovnarkom）提交报告，宣称“我们现在不需要正义，现在是你死我活的战争”，人民委员会以第21号会议**第9项内部行政决定**（非公开发布的国家法令）批准成立“全俄肃反委员会（ВЧК）”；直至1918年11月前无成文章程（Leggett pp. 17–18, `CLM-leggett-1981-vecheka-extra-legal-origins`）。契卡挂靠人民委员会以规避全俄中执委多党监督；列宁修正案架空施坦因贝格对政治逮捕的事先同意权（Leggett pp. 21–22, 47–49）。1918年上半年亚历山德罗维奇等左翼社革党副主席对政治处决行使否决权（Leggett pp. 49–59; Rabinowitch pp. 274–282）。**1918-07-07至08-22** 米尔巴赫遇刺后捷尔任斯基停职受查，**彼得斯** 代理主席至8月22日法令恢复其职务（Leggett pp. 74–77）。
- **左翼社革党清洗、红色恐怖制度化与“国中之国”确立（1918.07–1921）**：`1918-07-06` 刺杀米尔巴赫事件后，契卡于7月7日枪决副主席亚历山德罗维奇等13名左翼社革党人，清除内部制动阀（Leggett pp. 59, 77）。`1918-08-30` 乌里茨基遇刺与列宁遇刺后，人民委员会于 `1918-09-05` 颁布《关于红色恐怖的法令》（Leggett pp. 108–110）。俄共（布）中央 `1919-02-08` 公开信宣称契卡“完全是作为党的直接机关而创立、存在和工作的”，`1919-03-16` 捷尔任斯基兼任内务人民委员（Leggett pp. 144, 151, 358–359）。
- **1922年改组为 GPU/OGPU 与“恐怖入法”（1922–1926）**：`1922-02-06` 全俄中执委法令废除 ВЧК，在内务人民委员部下设 **GPU**，**内务人民委员兼 GPU 主席仍由捷尔任斯基出任**（组织换牌、首脑未变）。列宁 `1922-05-17` 致库尔斯基指令“恐怖入法”、条文尽可能宽泛（Leggett pp. 344–347）。`1923-07-06` 苏联宪法确立全联盟 GPU；**`1923-11-15`** 全苏中执委与苏联人民委员会批准 **《国家政治保卫总局条例》**，ОГПУ 成为直属苏联人民委员会的常设宪制机关（Leggett pp. 346–352）。1923年9月起监控党内反对派。
- **门任斯基时代：党内反对派镇压、强迫劳动营扩张与强制集体化尖兵（1926–1934）**：`1926-07-20` 捷尔任斯基猝死后，维亚切斯拉夫·门任斯基（V. R. Menzhinsky）继任 OGPU 主席（雅戈达任实权副主席）。1927年底至1929年，OGPU 直接执行对托洛茨基—季诺维也夫联合反对派的搜查、逮捕、流放与驱逐出境，并在1928年制造“沙赫特案”技术专家审判；1928–1933年在大转变与全盘集体化运动中，OGPU 成为执行乌拉尔—西伯利亚非常征粮、“消灭富农阶级”大流放及组建古拉格（GULAG，1930年正式设局）强迫劳动体系的核心暴力支柱，直至 `1934-07-10` 并入全联盟内务人民委员部（NKVD）国家安全总局（Leggett pp. 351–363; Khlevniuk pp. 97–115; Kotkin ch. 14）。

### 2.3 史学争鸣光谱（Historiographical Spectrum）
- **经验共识**：全俄契卡于1917年12月凭人民委员会行政决议成立，集侦查、逮捕、审判与行刑大权于一身；1918年7月清洗左翼社革党后于9月正式启动制度化“红色恐怖”；1922–1923年改组为 GPU/OGPU 后成为写进苏联宪法的常设国家机关。
- **主要解释分歧**（详参 `DEB-1918-war-communism-ideology-or-emergency`）：
  - **[制度史与警察国家连续性派 / George Leggett, Leonard Schapiro]** (`CLM-leggett-1981-vecheka-extra-legal-origins`, `CLM-leggett-1981-red-terror-institutionalization`, `CLM-leggett-1981-cheka-to-gpu-continuity`): 通过详实的法令与机构考据指出：（1）契卡直属人民委员会而非内务部或中执委，在宪制上复归了沙皇尼古拉一世直属皇室办公厅“第三厅”的专断传统，且其杀戮规模与法外特权远超沙皇奥赫拉那（列宁执政6年间契卡直接处决约14万人，镇压起义与集中营致死又至少14万人）；（2）红色恐怖在时序上早于协约国实质武装干涉，源于列宁的阶级消灭学说；（3）1922年改组为 GPU 是掩人耳目的政治伪装，在列宁“恐怖入法”指令下迅速恢复法外特权。Leggett 同时审慎区分了“技术前提”与“历史必然性”——斯大林的大清洗并非列宁统治的必然宿命，但正是列宁打造的契卡—OGPU 警察国家机器使斯大林的强制集体化、古拉格与大清洗在技术上成为可能。
  - **[自由-极权主义内生决定论 / Richard Pipes]** (`CLM-pipes-1990-red-terror-leninist-logic`): 认为法国大革命以恐怖告终，而俄国十月革命“以恐怖开始”；布尔什维克在尚无有组织武装反抗的1917年12月即建立契卡，并在1920年内战胜利后拒不废除，证明恐怖绝非战时应急手段，而是少数派政权弥补缺乏民意支持的“永久替代物（surrogate for popular support）”与废法专政（rule unrestricted by any law）的本质体现。
  - **[危机演进与地方情境修正派 / Alexander Rabinowitch]** (`CLM-rabinowitch-2007-contingency-of-dictatorship`, `CLM-rabinowitch-2007-left-sr-coalition-collapse`): 基于彼得格勒党政与契卡档案反驳“自夺权首日即按极权蓝图行事”的先验决定论，指出在1918年上半年彼得格勒，由于乌里茨基、克列斯廷斯基与左翼社革党内务委员普罗相的联合节制，彼得格勒契卡长期避免政治处决（甚至在4月和6月两度被讨论废除）；直到《布列斯特和约》分裂、严重饥荒、合格党员枯竭、左翼社革党于7月被清洗及协约国干涉引发极端“围城心态（siege mentality）”，基层区委自下而上的压力才与中央指令汇合，在8月30日乌里茨基遇刺后引爆系统性红色恐怖。

### 3.2 核心论断卡与专著原文直引档案
- **创立决议的非立法性质与沙皇“第三厅”宪制类比**（George Leggett, *The Cheka: Lenin's Political Police*, 1981, `ch01`, `CLM-leggett-1981-vecheka-extra-legal-origins`）：
  > "In his speech, describing the internal dangers confronting the Revolution, he [Dzerzhinsky] said: '... Do not think that I seek forms of revolutionary justice; we are not now in need of justice. It is war now—face to face, a fight to the finish. Life or death! I propose, I demand an organ for the revolutionary settlement of accounts with the counter-revolutionaries. And we must act not tomorrow, but today, now. ...'" `[p. 17]`
  > "Half-hearted Soviet attempts to represent this Sovnarkom resolution as a decree have long since been abandoned. ... It was never published as a decree. ... Writing in 1924, Krylenko, Chairman of the Supreme Revolutionary Tribunal, remarked: 'Up to November 1918 it [the Vecheka] existed without any statutes or laws.'" `[p. 18]`
  > "Constitutionally, this relationship harked back to the tradition of the Okhrana's precursor, the Third Section of Nicholas I's Chancellery, directly subordinated to the arbitrary control of the autocracy. By contrast, the Okhrana itself had stood modestly under the more formal and accountable authority of the Police Department of the Ministry of the Interior." `[p. 22]`
- **1918年9月5日《关于红色恐怖的法令》、拉齐斯阶级灭绝论与1919年2月党中央公开信**（Leggett 1981, `ch04`–`ch06`, `CLM-leggett-1981-red-terror-institutionalization`）：
  > "The classic statement of this fanatical attitude was formulated by Latsis, Chairman of the Eastern Front Cheka, writing in November 1918 in that Cheka's periodical, *Krasnyi Terror* (Red Terror): 'We are not waging war against individual persons. We are exterminating the bourgeoisie as a class. During the investigation, do not look for evidence that the accused acted in deed or word against Soviet power. The first questions that you ought to put are: To what class does he belong? What is his origin? What is his education or profession? And it is these questions that ought to determine the fate of the accused. In this lies the significance and essence of the Red Terror.'" `[p. 114]`
  > "After describing the counter-revolutionary forces ranged against the newly born Soviet regime, the Central Committee declared [8 February 1919]: '... The need for a special organ of merciless summary justice was recognised by our whole Party from top to bottom. Our Party entrusted this task to the Vecheka, equipping it with extraordinary plenary powers, and placing it in direct contact with the Party centre.' ... '... forgetting that the Chekas were founded, exist and work solely as the direct organs of the Party, under its direction and supervision.'" `[p. 144]`
- **1922年列宁“恐怖入法”密信与契卡—GPU—OGPU 极权警察国家奠基论**（Leggett 1981, `ch12`–`ch13`, `CLM-leggett-1981-cheka-to-gpu-continuity`）：
  > "Two days later [17 May 1922], Lenin sent Kurskii a draft paragraph for the envisaged Criminal Code, and wrote: 'The law should not abolish terror: to promise that would be self-delusion or deception; it should be substantiated and legalised in principle, clearly, without evasion or embellishment. The paragraph on terror should be formulated as widely as possible, since only revolutionary consciousness of justice and revolutionary conscience can determine the conditions of its application in practice.'" `[p. 347]`
  > "Equally, without the Vecheka's successors, the OGPU and the NKVD, Stalin's inhuman compulsory collectivisation of agriculture, his vast system of concentration camp forced labour, and the mass purges of the thirties, could not have happened: a police state, backed by terror, was an essential pre-condition for the occurrence of all three phenomena. This savage aftermath of Lenin's rule was by no means its inevitable consequence, but it was Lenin who laid the police-state foundations which made Stalin's monstrous feats technically possible." `[p. 362]`
- **派普斯关于恐怖作为“民意替补物”的论断与拉宾诺维奇关于彼得格勒早期抑制机制的修正**（`CLM-pipes-1990-red-terror-leninist-logic`, `CLM-rabinowitch-2007-contingency-of-dictatorship`）：
  > "As it had been for the Jacobins, terror served the Bolsheviks not as a weapon of last resort, but as a surrogate for the popular support which eluded them." `[Pipes 1990, ch. 18]`
  > "Contrary to conventional wisdom, the Red Terror that exploded in Petrograd on 30 August was not the consequence of a nationwide political crackdown inspired by Lenin and orchestrated by the VCheka... The burden of existing evidence indicates that these events can best be understood as the culmination of a gradual process during which the moderating influence of such key individuals as Uritskii, Krestinskii, and Proshian was replaced by pressure for systematic Red Terror, in part 'from below.'" `[Rabinowitch 2007, p. 398 / PDF p. 421]`
