## [T1] 60秒定性微摘要
1917年底从社会革命党分裂出的激进民粹派政党（1917–1923），以斯皮里多诺娃、普罗相等为首。拥护苏维埃政权并于1917年12月加入人民委员会与布尔什维克联合执政；1918年3月因反《布列斯特和约》退阁但仍维持北方公社合作，7月6日刺杀德国大使米尔巴赫遭全面清洗而覆灭。

## [T2] 核心脉络、时效关系边表与史学争鸣光谱

### 2.1 核心历史脉络
- **社革党内反战左翼崛起、十月留席与正式独立建党（1917.03–1917.12）**：1917年二月革命后，以玛丽亚·斯皮里多诺娃（Maria Spiridonova）、鲍里斯·卡姆科夫（Boris Kamkov）、马克·纳坦松（Mark Natanson）、普罗什·普罗相（Prosh Proshian）及伊萨克·施坦因贝格（Isaac Steinberg）为首的社会革命党左翼国际主义派，猛烈抨击切尔诺夫、戈茨等党中央中右翼支持临时政府继续帝国主义战争、推迟将土地移交土地委员会以及在七月事变后恢复前线死刑。`1917-10-25 [O.S.] / 1917-11-07 [N.S.]` 全俄苏维埃二大开幕夜，当右翼社革党与孟什维克退场抗议时，179名左翼社革党代表决定留在斯莫尔尼宫，投票拥护苏维埃政权与吸纳社革党242份农民委托书的《土地法令》，进入全俄中央执委会（VTsIK），但最初为促成全社会主义政党联合政府（维克热尔谈判）而暂拒单独加入人民委员会（Rabinowitch pp. 18–29）。遭社革党中央开除后，左翼社革党于 `1917-11-19至11-28 [O.S.] / 1917-12-02至12-11 [N.S.]` 在彼得格勒召开第一次代表大会，正式成立独立的 **左翼社会革命党（国际主义者）（ПЛСР）**。
- **两党联合人民委员会、解散立宪会议与契卡双头制衡（1917.12–1918.03）**：经与布尔什维克反复谈判，左翼社革党于 `1917-11-15 [O.S.]` 达成工农兵苏维埃合并协议，并于 `1917-12-09/12 [O.S.] / 1917-12-22/25 [N.S.]` 正式加入人民委员会（Sovnarkom），执掌农业（科列加耶夫 A. L. Kolegaev）、司法（施坦因贝格 I. Z. Steinberg）、邮政电报（普罗相 P. P. Proshian）、地方自治（特鲁托夫斯基 V. E. Trutovsky）、国家财产（卡列林 V. A. Karelin）等人民委员部，同时于1918年1月派出亚历山德罗维奇（V. A. Aleksandrovich）等出任全俄肃反委员会（契卡）副主席及委员（Rabinowitch pp. 50–80; Leggett pp. 44–51）。在 `1918-01-05/06 [O.S.] / 1918-01-18/19 [N.S.]` 立宪会议危机中，左翼社革党坚定站在布尔什维克一边，以“苏维埃高于资产阶级议会”为由共同武力解散了右翼社革党占多数的立宪会议，并在全俄苏维埃三大上共同促成《土地社会化基本法》的颁布（`CLM-rabinowitch-2007-constituent-assembly`, pp. 113–127）。同时，施坦因贝格与契卡内的左翼社革党代表对政治犯死刑坚持行使一票否决权，成为1918年上半年遏制大规模政治处决的关键制度制动阀（`CLM-leggett-1981-vecheka-extra-legal-origins`, `CLM-leggett-1981-red-terror-institutionalization`, pp. 47–59）。
- **《布列斯特和约》退阁与彼得格勒“北方公社”地方联合执政试验（1918.03–1918.06）**：围绕是否接受德国苛刻割地条件，左翼社革党狂热主张以“革命游击战争”抵抗帝国主义；`1918-03-15` 第四次非常全俄苏维埃代表大会批准《布列斯特和约》后，左翼社革党人民委员集体辞职退出中央政府，但声明继续留在全俄中执委、各级苏维埃与契卡等机构内支持苏维埃政权。Rabinowitch 的档案研究揭示，尽管莫斯科中央关系在5–6月布尔什维克推行“粮食独裁”、派遣武装征粮队（`продотряды`）与设立贫农委员会（`комбеды`）挑起农村阶级战争后急剧恶化，但在迁都后的彼得格勒及新组建的 **北方州公社联盟（SK SO，北方公社）** 中，以普罗相（任北方公社内务人民委员兼彼得格勒军区政委）、科尔尼洛夫（农业委员）为首的左翼社革党人与季诺维也夫、乌里茨基、克列斯廷斯基等彼得格勒布尔什维克直至1918年7月初仍保持着高效、温和且极具建设性的联合执政——不仅填补了布尔什维克严酷的干部断层，而且在普罗相与乌里茨基配合下成功抵制了在彼得格勒发动红色恐怖（甚至在4月和6月两度推动撤销彼得格勒契卡）（`CLM-rabinowitch-2007-left-sr-coalition-collapse`, pp. 265–282, 396）。
- **全俄苏维埃五大选票舞弊、1918年7月6日刺杀米尔巴赫“政治自杀”与一党专政确立（1918.06–1923）**：1918年6月党员逾10万人，本可在 **`1918-07-04`** 开幕的**第五次全俄苏维埃代表大会**（法定代表约 **1,164名**，两党合法选出代表原本旗鼓相当）上争取政策转向；斯维尔德洛夫等人塞入约 **399名** 资格存疑代表，制造 **678:269** 虚假多数（`CLM-rabinowitch-2007-left-sr-coalition-collapse`, p. 288）。`1918-07-06` 布柳姆金刺杀米尔巴赫后，波波夫分队扣押捷尔任斯基与拉齐斯、占领电报局、与拉脱维亚步兵交火并炮击克里姆林宫周边——属武装暴动但并非预谋推翻苏维埃的全盘起义（Rabinowitch pp. 291–295）。列宁借机大清洗；彼得格勒贵胄学校案逮捕约150–161人，**未见拉宾诺维奇所载“打死10人”伤亡数**。左翼社革党分裂为民粹派共产主义者、革命共产主义者（并入俄共）与斯皮里多诺娃派，多党苏维埃联合执政终结（Rabinowitch pp. 308–309, 395–396）。

### 2.3 史学争鸣光谱（Historiographical Spectrum）
- **经验共识**：左翼社会革命党是苏俄历史上唯一曾与布尔什维克正式组成中央联合政府（1917年12月—1918年3月）的社会主义政党；两党在1918年1月合力解散立宪会议，在3月因《布列斯特和约》与5–6月因农村征粮和贫农委员会政策发生激烈冲突，最终于1918年7月6–7日米尔巴赫遇刺事件后彻底破裂，标志着苏俄从多党苏维埃政体转向布尔什维克一党专政。
- **主要解释分歧**（详参 `DEB-1917-october-coup-or-social-revolution`、`DEB-1918-war-communism-ideology-or-emergency`）：
  - **[后苏联地方档案与演进偶然性派 / Alexander Rabinowitch]** (`CLM-rabinowitch-2007-constituent-assembly`, `CLM-rabinowitch-2007-contingency-of-dictatorship`, `CLM-rabinowitch-2007-left-sr-coalition-collapse`): 提出三项颠覆性修正：（1）**地方多党合作的真实生命力**：以彼得格勒及“北方公社”为例，证明布尔什维克与左翼社革党在1918年3月退阁后仍在地方内务、农业、军区与司法层面保持高度建设性的伙伴关系并成功遏制了契卡恐怖，合作潜力远未耗尽；（2）**苏维埃五大选票舞弊的逼迫效应**：左翼社革党之所以在7月6日铤而走险刺杀米尔巴赫，直接导火索是布尔什维克捏造约399名代表资格、用欺诈手段剥夺了左翼社革党在五大上本应享有的合法多数，堵死了苏维埃民主纠错渠道；（3）**“非反苏武装起义”定性**：7月6–7日左翼社革党人仅试图挑起对德战争以迫使列宁改变政策，绝无推翻布尔什维克夺权之企图，所谓“武装暴动”系列宁借机在全国（含毫不知情的彼得格勒）发动先发制人清洗、铲除最大政治对手的政治定性。
  - **[自由-极权主义工具论 / Richard Pipes, George Leggett]** (`CLM-pipes-1990-october-classic-coup`, `CLM-leggett-1981-vecheka-extra-legal-origins`, `CLM-leggett-1981-red-terror-institutionalization`): 将左翼社革党视为政治上幼稚、被列宁暂时利用以分裂农民阵线并替解散立宪会议背书的“过河拆桥”对象；Leggett 指出，列宁在1917年12月修改施坦因贝格草案时已确保布尔什维克对契卡的实质掌控，一旦左翼社革党妨碍粮食征集与一党垄断，清洗便不可避免。
  - **[苏联官方正统叙事]**：将1917年底与左翼社革党的合作解释为争取中农的暂时妥协，将1918年5–7月的决裂定性为富农利益代言人抗拒农村社会主义革命（贫农委员会），并将7月6–7日事件定性为“左翼社革党旨在推翻苏维埃政权的反革命武装叛乱”。

### 3.2 核心论断卡与专著原文直引档案
- **1918年1月解散立宪会议中的左翼社革党角色**（Alexander Rabinowitch, *The Bolsheviks in Power: The First Year of Soviet Rule in Petrograd*, 2007, `ch08`, `CLM-rabinowitch-2007-constituent-assembly`）：
  > "Certainly, contributing to this result was the Bolsheviks' strong popular support in the Petrograd region, as reflected in the mid-November elections to the Constituent Assembly, and the SR leadership's rejection of efforts to provide military security for the Constituent Assembly coupled with the Bolsheviks' and Left SRs' readiness to resort to force of arms to defend Soviet power. Most important, however, Sviatitskii was probably on target when he pointed to the Russian people's fundamental indifference to the fate of the Constituent Assembly, allowing Lenin to command that they all simply go home." `[p. 127 / PDF p. 150]`
- **全俄苏维埃五大选票造假、7月6日米尔巴赫事件非起义性质与北方公社合作遗产**（Rabinowitch 2007, `ch17` & `ch22`, `CLM-rabinowitch-2007-left-sr-coalition-collapse`）：
  > "There is, in fact, substantial circumstantial evidence that the huge Bolshevik majority in the congress was fabricated, and that the number of legitimately elected Left SR delegates was roughly equal to that of the Bolsheviks... Unfortunately for the Left SRs, these tallies did not include roughly 399 Bolshevik delegates whose right to be seated was challenged by the Left SR minority in the congress's credentials commission." `[p. 288 / PDF p. 311]`
  > "By the night of 6 July, the actions of the Left SRs, beginning with Count Mirbach's assassination, were already being defined by the Soviet government as 'an uprising against Soviet power,' and historians have often depicted the episode that way. But is this characterization valid? Having sifted through the available published and unpublished evidence, I conclude that it was not. Upon investigation, with the possible exception of Prosh Proshian's brief occupation of the Central Telegraph Office and his behavior there, which may well have been unauthorized, all of the Moscow Left SRs' actions following Mirbach's assassination were consistent with the objective of reshaping the policies of the Leninist Sovnarkom but not with forcibly seizing power or even fighting the Bolsheviks except in self-defense." `[p. 294 / PDF p. 317]`
  > "The Soviet government's first official announcement about Mirbach's assassination accused the Left SRs of giving the Germans the best possible excuse to attack Russia. But by the same token, the Left SRs' act, however understandable framed against the fraudulent composition of the Fifth All-Russian Congress of Soviets and the ominous developments at the congress's start, offered Lenin a better excuse than he could possibly have hoped for to eliminate the Left SRs as a significant political rival. He took full advantage of the opportunity in Moscow and also pressed leaders in key political centers such as Petrograd, where the Bolshevik–Left SR partnership endured, to launch preemptive strikes against the Left SRs and to neutralize them for good as a significant political force." `[pp. 308–309 / PDF pp. 331–332]`
  > "Looking back on the development of the Bolshevik–Left SR partnership nationally, it is fair to conclude that its breakup had historical ramifications beyond marking the moment when the Soviet political system became a one-party dictatorship. The Left SRs provided Soviet power with a critically important link to the countryside. Had the Bolshevik–Left SR alliance survived, it seems likely that the Russian civil war would have been significantly less torturous. ... It is also true, however, that in Petrograd the Bolshevik–Left SR alliance at all levels of city and regional government operated reasonably well and had not exhausted its potential when it was shattered by events in Moscow." `[p. 396 / PDF p. 419]`
